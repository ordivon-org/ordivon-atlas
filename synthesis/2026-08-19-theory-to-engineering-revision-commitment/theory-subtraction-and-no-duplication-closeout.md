## 1. Component-wise theory subtraction

The umbrella candidate was compared against mature theory families rather than protected by new terminology.

Strong ancestral coverage was found for:

- **model-based diagnosis / sequential diagnosis** — discrepancy localization and discriminating measurements;
- **meta-diagnosis** — model, observations or diagnostic machinery can themselves be faulty;
- **hierarchical diagnosis / abstraction** — multiple diagnostic levels and task-dependent granularity;
- **AGM/belief revision and ontology debugging/revision** — minimal-change contraction/revision and representation repair;
- **problem reformulation / model invalidation** — parameter fit can fail because the model family itself is inadequate;
- **rational metareasoning / value of computation / value of information** — whether further reasoning/evidence is worth delaying external action;
- **bounded rationality / satisficing** — finite agents need not solve globally optimal decision problems before acting;
- **dual control / active learning control** — actions can both control and generate information;
- **BDI / intention reconsideration / belief-intention revision** — rational commitment and reopening;
- **safe RL / constrained control** — capability can coexist with restricted exploration/action;
- **truth-maintenance / ATMS** — dependency/assumption/justification preservation;
- **multi-agent and trust-sensitive belief revision** — source/domain-relative epistemic change;
- **Multi-Context Systems / managed MCS** — heterogeneous autonomous contexts linked without one uniform global logic;
- **PolicyMaker, RT, SecPAL, DKAL and related authorization systems** — localized authority, delegation, query-time authorization, revocation/current-state concerns;
- **Input/Output logic and normative logical architectures** — obligations/permissions/constraints need not collapse into truth;
- **argumentation-based practical reasoning** — heterogeneous goals, values, norms and reasons can justify action without one scalar utility.

Consequence:

No individual component justified Ordivon novelty for `Finite Situated Intelligence Dynamics` or `Praxis`.

## 3. Final residual: typed multi-authority decision composition

The last candidate residual was narrow:

How can a decision consume non-comparable epistemic, normative, authority, capability and currentness/provenance standings from autonomous owners without a Supreme Global Truth Owner?

This also encountered strong prior-art families and, more importantly, failed an Ordivon engineering deletion test.

The useful operational non-collapse remains:

- epistemic standing controls what claims/premises may be relied upon;
- normative standing supplies obligations/permissions/prohibitions or other owner-native normative relations;
- authority standing controls who may authorize/delegate;
- capability standing controls what can actually be realized;
- currentness/provenance controls whether a standing is consumable for the present decision.

These roles are not one scalar score, one universal constraint set or one universal Standing enum.
