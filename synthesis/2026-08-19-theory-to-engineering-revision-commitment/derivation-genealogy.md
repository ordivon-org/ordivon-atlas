# Derivation Genealogy — From Theory-to-Engineering Translation to Bounded Revision/Commitment

## Status

This file preserves **historical derivation scaffolding**. The labels below are retrieval handles, not admitted Foundations, owner semantics or universal theory primitives.

## 1. Initial problem

The starting pressure was practical:

`ResearchResult -> ? -> EngineeringChange`.

The central correction was that theory does not compile directly into code. Research can instead change engineering through distinctions, constraints, exposed evidence, owner/boundary structure, mechanism generation and route elimination.

A canonical motivating repair was the Network line:

`Communicability -> InterLocusCapability`.

The repair showed that reachability/communicability cannot silently stand in for capability, authority or admissibility.

## 2. D1–D9 historical scaffolds

The conversation expanded nine provisional scaffolds:

1. **D1 Research–Engineering Constraint** — research modifies the engineering possibility space through explicit bridge premises rather than direct prescription.
2. **D2 Constrained Intervention** — design is intervention search under hard constraints, preferences, assumptions, objectives and finite resources.
3. **D3 Translation Admission** — `ResearchStanding != TranslationStanding`; frozen research does not automatically become an engineering MUST.
4. **D4 Semantic Realization** — representation must survive materialization, transition, persistence, recovery and observation; rich ontology with legacy causal control is a semantic shadow implementation.
5. **D5 Compositional Realization** — correct components do not imply correct composition; no boundary may silently strengthen historical/current, reachable/authorized or evidence/claim standing.
6. **D6 Operational Epistemic Feedback** — observation is not evidence; operational outcomes must be localized to theory/translation/intervention/implementation/composition/validation layers before revision.
7. **D7 Adaptive Intervention Synthesis** — intervention programmes are evidence-responsive policies, not fixed sequences; bottlenecks and complements migrate.
8. **D8 Normative Objective Mediation** — `Can != Should != May != Must`; intent/objectives require their own contextual interpretation and authority.
9. **D9 Typed Standing Composition** — epistemic, normative, authority and capability roles are not one scalar standing and should not be laundered across roles.

These scaffolds were later contracted because keeping nine peer theories duplicated structure and encouraged abstraction inflation.

## 3. First contraction — K0–K3

### K0 — Typed Situated Standing

A thin shared grammar was used for reasoning:

`standing = role + content + owner + scope + provenance + currentness/strength information`.

This was never meant to become a universal database row. Owner-native semantics remain authoritative.

### K1 — Situated Frontier Formation

Current typed standings shape a decision-relative admissible frontier:

`Sigma_t -> F_t`.

Research translation, normative constraints, authority composition and capability facts are different contributors to frontier formation, not one truth role.

### K2 — Adaptive Intervention

Judgment was reframed as selecting an intervention under the current frontier, uncertainty, resources and objectives. Candidate actions include measurement, experiment, prototype, build, delegation, rollback, waiting and stopping.

### K3 — Reality Reconciliation

Interventions act on Reality; observations become evidence only through an interpretation lineage; evidence can revise standings and eventually the representation itself.

The minimal loop was:

`standing -> frontier -> intervention -> Reality -> evidence -> revised standing`.

## 4. Possibility and representation expansion

The inquiry then moved upstream of action selection: where do candidate possibilities come from?

Useful non-collapse:

`OnticPossible != Representable != Conceivable != Constructible != Admissible != Chosen != Realized`.

The conversation explored materially new possibility as changes in mechanism, boundary, representation, composition, information flow, authority structure, achievable state, evidence route or cost structure.

This led to **Representation Escape**: a failure may lie not in search inside the current representation but in the representation's inability to express a load-bearing distinction.

Historical escape operators included Split, Merge, Re-axis, Boundary Escape, Role Separation, Relation Promotion, Temporalization/Contextualization, Counterfactual Re-description and cross-domain representation transport.

Again, these are operator families/heuristics, not admitted ontology.

## 5. Epistemic attractors and permeability

A stronger reflexive problem then appeared:

`Representation -> Measurement -> Evidence -> Representation`.

Current representations can influence instruments, schemas, standards, training, institutional selection and interventions, thereby shaping the future evidence distribution.

A **self-sealing epistemic attractor** was provisionally described as a knowledge structure that lowers the probability that counterevidence or alternative representations can be generated, preserved, recognized or adopted.

The practical survivor was **epistemic permeability**: maintain routes by which Reality, anomalies and rival representations can genuinely change standing.

This did **not** become an independent theory claim.

## 6. Escape Search / Unknown-Unknown Contact

Because an unknown unknown cannot be directly enumerated, the problem was reframed from finding the missing concept to increasing the chance that unmodelled structure creates an observable residual.

Historical contact channels included:

- mechanistically different environments;
- independent instrumentation/evaluator lineages;
- boundary/scale/time perturbation;
- separating worlds/counterfactual fixtures;
- anomaly retention and later reinterpretation;
- open-world consequences;
- cross-domain/historical representation contact;
- owner-local semantic friction that is not prematurely collapsed.

The term `Unknown-Unknown Contact Surface` was only a descriptive handle for this property, not a registry/object proposal.

## 7. Deep common-mode blindness

Multiple Agents/tools/representations may still share a deeper failure ancestry. Therefore:

`AgentCount != EpistemicIndependence`.

A durable question from this phase was:

`What do all current rivals agree on without testing?`

Shared residuals across rival models can justify pressure on the common representational family. However, no finite system can prove the absence of all deeper common-mode assumptions.

This forced the next contraction toward bounded rather than complete rationality.

## 8. Bounded epistemic rationality

The key action principle became:

`Do not require complete knowledge; require commitment-appropriate justification.`

Low standing does not always imply no action. It can imply a lower commitment class: inspect, probe, simulate, reversible experiment, bounded dogfood rather than irreversible mutation.

Reversibility, recoverability and option preservation reduce the standing required for bounded experimentation because they reduce irreversible commitment.

The important distinction became:

`UNKNOWN -> restrict commitment class`, not `UNKNOWN -> stop cognition`.

## 9. Second contraction — F0–F3

The epistemic expansion was compressed again:

### F0 — Representation & Contact

`(R_t, C_t)` — how the system represents Reality and which observation/contact regime exposes it.

### F1 — Typed Standing & Frontier

`Sigma_t -> F_t` — current typed standings shape the situated admissible frontier.

### F2 — Calibrated Commitment

Choose only a commitment that current standing, consequence, reversibility, authority and recovery justify.

### F3 — Reality Coupling & Revision

Intervention changes Reality and sometimes the future observation regime; new evidence can revise claims, mechanisms or representation.

Historical descriptive loop:

`(R_t,C_t) -> possibilities -> standings -> frontier -> intervention -> (Reality,contact) -> evidence -> revision`.

## 10. Dogfood repair — Failure Locus is not Revision Depth

The umbrella was not kept as-is. Four real Ordivon episodes showed that superficially similar anomalies can live at different loci:

- representation;
- contact/evidence fidelity;
- standing/currentness;
- commitment/admission.

Therefore the useful diagnostic survivor became:

`Locate failure -> choose minimal revision -> calibrate commitment`.

The strongest methodological addition was **Revision Selectivity**: good judgment includes refusing unnecessary abstraction/revision.

## 11. Current standing

The D/K/F labels are now **genealogy only**.

They should not be used to justify:

- a new Praxis or Finite-Intelligence owner;
- a universal decision service;
- a universal Standing table/schema;
- a persistent decision envelope;
- automatic ontology escalation whenever an anomaly appears.

The only live residual from this lineage is the separately preregistered Revision–Commitment Triage audit grammar, which must demonstrate prospective incremental value before receiving stronger standing.
