# Anti-Rediscovery Guard

## Purpose

Prevent future Agents from re-minting rejected or already-absorbed variants of the 2026-08-19 capability-formation theory while preserving the genuinely open later problem.

- Consequence Closure Principle

The source task records why these were absorbed by cybernetics/control, system identification, active learning, causal inference/transportability, evolutionary epistemology, organizational learning, resilience/viability, continual learning, MDL/statistical learning, real options, architectural-assumption management, technical debt, TMS/model-based diagnosis and controllability.

## Distinctions that survived and may be reused

Reuse does **not** imply novelty:

- `Feedback != Learning`
- repeated experience != discriminating information / identification
- consequence != correct attribution
- adaptation != normative correctness
- capability existence != local capability-formation history
- learning != retention
- retention != currentness
- `Standing != OperationalInfluence`
- `OperationalInfluence != Deactivation`
- intervention/update reach != change cost
- historical validity != current standing
- Reality supplies constraints; the learning/selection architecture performs the structural update/compression
- transferred artifacts may carry selected capability without carrying enough selection context for currentness/rechallenge

## Do not block the later open problem

The following is **not** closed by the rejected capability theory:

- whether Human knowledge-space is only one contingent projection of Reality;
- whether multiple low-overlap representations can solve overlapping external tasks;
- whether current problem/question generators systematically omit important regions;
- whether representation/search/evaluation structures create challenge-fragile epistemic basins;
- whether the topology of a problem-solution search space changes under different representational bases.

Key guard:

`NOVELTY_CLOSED != REFERENT_CLOSED`.

Do not use prior-art absorption as permission to suppress a new, precisely formulated anomaly about the underlying referent.

## Reopen conditions for the rejected broad theory

Reopen independent-theory standing only if a precise residual result survives mature prior-art subtraction and produces a nontrivial cross-domain invariant/prediction that cannot be reduced to the known component theories.
