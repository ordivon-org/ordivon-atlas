# Bottom-Up Reconstruction of Ordivon Research

## Status

`non-authoritative-methodology-synthesis`

Primary source phases: `task:ordivon-research-methodology-observation-20260819@15-17` plus the negative-theory witness `task:ordivon-consequence-selected-capability-theory-20260819@6`.

## Core correction

The conversation rejected the model:

`Research -> Original Theory / Foundation`.

A more useful interpretation emerged:

`Research = transformation of an epistemic state under evidence and reality constraints`.

The improvement need not increase primitive/theory count.

## Legitimate terminal outcomes

Research may correctly terminate as:

- `ORIGINAL_RESULT`
- `IMPORTED_MATURE_THEORY`
- `PROBLEM_RECONSTRUCTION`
- `CROSS_DOMAIN_SYNTHESIS`
- `ABSORBED_BY_PRIOR_ART`
- `DERIVED_ONLY`
- `BOUNDARY_REPAIR`
- `NO_CHANGE`
- `JUSTIFIED_STOP`
- `OPERATIONALIZATION / DOGFOOD`

These labels are analytical; they are not admitted Research System schema.

## Provisional production chain

`Question/Anomaly`

`-> Referent / Problem Reconstruction`

`-> Internal Prior-Art Recovery`

`-> External Mature-Theory Recovery / Subtraction`

`-> Rival / Residual Formation`

`-> Discriminating Investigation`

`-> Evidence / Evaluation`

`-> Standing Update`

`-> Integration / Materialization`

`-> Consumption`

`-> Rechallenge / Recovery`

The key repair added later is that mature-theory subtraction may close **novelty**, not necessarily the underlying **referent question**.

## Three-space analytical lens

The conversation also separated:

1. **Referent Space** — what object/domain/process is being studied;
2. **Epistemic / Method Capability Space** — how relevant distinctions can be observed, inferred, proved or falsified;
3. **Intervention / Realization Space** — what can be constructed, perturbed, executed, migrated, rolled back or otherwise tested.

This is an explanatory lens only. It must not become Atlas schema by narrative inertia.

## Why the failed theory search matters

The capability-theory search is a strong concrete witness that research can improve Ordivon even when an independent-theory claim dies:

- prior-art search eliminated false novelty;
- false general laws were rejected;
- typed distinctions survived;
- one boundary improved Standing / Currentness;
- the negative result changed how Ordivon interprets research outputs;
- the next, deeper problem became visible.

Therefore:

`IndependentTheoryRejected != ResearchFailure`.

## Stability correction

Do not overcorrect into `good research = constant reframing`.

The Methodology episode corpus independently preserves stable/no-op/destructive-survival research. A good system should be selectively mutable: stable under insufficient challenge, revisable under sufficiently discriminating challenge.
