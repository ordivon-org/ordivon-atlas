# Open Problem Handoff — Problem / Representation / Solution Space Beyond Human Knowledge

## Standing

`OPEN_PROBLEM / DO_NOT_COLLAPSE_PREMATURELY`

This file does not assert a new theory or discipline. It preserves why a new independent conversation was warranted.

## Core doubt

Human knowledge is formed under particular biological cognition, senses, instruments, language, mathematics, institutions, historical paths and research methods.

Its empirical and engineering success does **not** logically establish that:

- Humans have generated the uniquely natural question set;
- current conceptual decompositions are uniquely privileged;
- current mature theories exhaust the important solution families;
- one external task has one privileged internal problem/solution representation;
- mature human coverage equals Reality coverage;
- a theory's historical/scientific maturity guarantees broad referent closure.

## Thought-experiment pressure

An independently developed epistemic civilization could in principle:

- converge with Humans on some invariants;
- use radically different internal primitives while solving overlapping external tasks;
- articulate regions Humans never formulated;
- omit structures Humans consider central;
- expose Human distinctions as coordinate artifacts;
- itself be trapped by different blind spots.

This is a counterfactual probe, not evidence that alien civilizations exist.

## Deeper structural possibility

The concerning object may not be merely a wrong answer. The problem/question/representation/search/evaluation structure itself may shape:

- which questions are expressible;
- which candidate answers exist in the model family;
- which candidates count as nearby/reachable;
- which evidence is generated;
- which solutions receive high score;
- which anomalies remain invisible or unformulated.

A locally stable scientific or computational basin could therefore be highly successful under its accessible evidence/criteria while remaining fragile under a wider challenge family.

## Explicit non-collapse instruction

Do not begin the independent inquiry by declaring it to be merely:

- scientific pluralism;
- underdetermination;
- epistemic landscapes;
- paradigm theory;
- representation learning;
- No-Free-Lunch;
- local optima;
- shortcut learning;
- open-ended search;
- any provisional Ordivon formalism from the parent conversation.

All may become relevant prior art later. None is permitted to define the problem before it is adequately discovered.

## First-stage objective

Discover the actual problem space:

- what distinct questions are currently mixed together;
- which concepts (`problem`, `solution`, `representation`, `equivalence`, `truth`, `coverage`, `unknown`) require repair;
- whether the question has meaningful formalizable substructures;
- where existing disciplines absorb parts of it and where they do not;
- whether unknown-question-space pressure can even be investigated without pretending to enumerate the unformulated.
