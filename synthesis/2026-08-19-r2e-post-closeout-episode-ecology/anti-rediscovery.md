# Anti-Rediscovery — R2E Post-Closeout Episode Ecology

## Do not reconstruct one R2E pipeline

The later evidence explicitly rejects treating Ordivon Research→Engineering as one monotone translation chain.

Do not infer:

- research result → engineering artifact by default;
- engineering target is fixed before research;
- low production output = low research value;
- more context/prior exposure = better reasoning;
- eventual reuse = timely ordinary-path reuse;
- a correct current non-admission is permanent invalidity.

## Do not rebuild rejected global remedies

Current evidence does **not** justify:

- Knowledge Activation layer/service/owner;
- global Context Composer or all-context injector;
- global owner/relevance registry or CandidateOwner generator;
- Agenda/Pressure/R2E controller;
- universal decision-local standing envelope;
- global delivery queue/synchronization daemon;
- TargetLifecycle/reopen registry;
- universal migration/rehome controller;
- scalar R2E/reuse/timing/relevance score;
- generic product schema for all owners;
- permanent Knowledge Topology/search/relation graph from importance or vocabulary recurrence alone.

Try owner-native/current surfaces and bounded consumer evidence first.

## Preserve the typed failure ecology

Do not collapse:

`RECOVERY_FAILED`

`DECISION_SURFACE_OMISSION`

`SEMANTIC_PROJECTION_LOSS`

`CONTEXTUAL_BINDING_FRAGILITY`

`PROTECTIVE_BLOCK / TERMINAL_CORRECT`

into one generic “knowledge not activated” diagnosis.

Likewise preserve:

`ResearchTruth != EngineeringProjection != OperationalReality`.

## Prior exposure has a non-monotonic cost

CF-B is a negative control against broad/default synthesis exposure.

CF-C is a negative control against assuming pointer invocation has incremental value.

Finance remediation is a positive control for a narrow deletion-essential distinction exposed at the exact decision surface.

Do not optimize for earliest or maximum prior injection.

## Preserve healthy low-materialization outcomes

Reference-only artifacts, deletion results, `NO_CHANGE`, `NOT_ADMITTED`, `NO_NEXT_BRANCH`, evidence waits and architecture subtraction can be correct outcomes.

Do not manufacture production work to increase visible conversion.

But do not make subtraction permanent by inertia either:

`CorrectNonAdmission(t0) != PermanentNonAdmission`.

Fresh evidence must be able to reopen the question without historical standing being silently restored.

## Keep permanence levels separate

`SharedResponsibilityPermanence != SharedRepresentationPermanence != SharedContentPermanence`.

`PermanentRecoveryPurpose != PermanentRecoveryMechanism`.

A responsibility can be institutionally permanent while its concrete index, relation view, alias set or local representation remains rebuildable/disposable.

## Inspect behavioral consumers after authority contraction

Computing project-registry repair establishes:

`DeclaredAuthorityContraction != BehavioralAuthorityContraction`.

When a carrier/view loses semantic scope, inspect actual code/consumer dependencies rather than updating documentation only.

Do not delete legitimate in-role consumers merely because some out-of-role consumers were wrong.

## Do not collapse “migration”

Keep separate:

- semantic owner split/rehome;
- durable authority-state-root rehome;
- single-writer cutover;
- compatibility reader/product retention;
- compatibility-path deletion;
- repository/source movement.

Host→Harness proves one Run needs one current durable authority root; H1 proves that does not require immediate deletion of every explicit legacy product path.

`AuthorityStateRootRehome != CompatibilityPathDeletion != RepositoryMove`.

## Atlas is not the whole truth universe

`AtlasCaptureCompleteness != OwnerSourceRecoverability != CurrentAnswerability`.

Missing federation of a relevant owner blocks only claims requiring that owner. Use direct owner-current recovery when known; do not launder synthesis/history into current authority and do not build federation by owner count.

## Reopen this R2E phase only from natural evidence

Useful reopen pressure includes:

- low-overlap owner recovery failure that existing bounded routes cannot repair;
- repeated ordinary-path consumer failure after current owner distinctions are correctly exposed;
- production re-admission under new consumer evidence;
- a physical migration/path constraint that authority contraction cannot solve;
- a concrete deletion-essential shared representation failure;
- a later consumer showing this synthesis itself has stale/misleading process memory.

Do not reopen because the task has conceptual room or because a global architecture would be elegant.
