## Coupling is typed

The audit distinguished several coupling forms because they have different engineering consequences:

- **Incubation coupling** — one repository temporarily hosts research whose semantic ownership is still unsettled.
- **Evidence coupling** — one corpus or system holds evidence useful to several owners.
- **Implementation coupling** — several semantic responsibilities share code/repository/transport.
- **Recovery coupling** — one surface helps locate or reconstruct another owner’s current authority.
- **Semantic-authority coupling** — a carrier/projection begins to imply truth outside its mandate.

The first three can be healthy. Recovery coupling requires currentness and source fences. Semantic-authority coupling is the primary destructive risk.

`RepositoryCoupling != SemanticAuthorityCoupling`

`CoordinationBurden != CoordinationWaste`
