# Cooper MedChem Diversification Hidden-Evidence Protocol v0.1

## Why this is stronger than historical literature replay

The 2024 workflow deposited machine-readable/raw artifacts from an actually executed autonomous experiment. We can blind sample identity and stage outcomes mechanically. This reduces dependence on a language model's historical-literature knowledge, although the benchmark designer still knows the published anomaly and must therefore remain outside the participant view.

## Critical policy-coupling receipt

The deposited `Example1_MedicinalChemistry.ipynb` was retrieved through a scoped HTTPS CONNECT path and its published md5 was verified (`92818b2f09ba4e221e4095a5e195b0d9`, 21,282 bytes).

Therefore this fixture directly instantiates:

`PhysicallyMeasured != Retained != PolicyCoupled`.

This matters because the later published unexpected cyclization was found by human inspection of NMR that the autonomous diversification decision policy did not use.

## R0 — incumbent autonomous standing

Expose, under opaque IDs:

- intended sample chemistry from the frozen stage input;
- expected MS adduct masses;
- processed UPLC-MS summaries;
- original UPLC-MS `MS_PASS` standing for each cohort member.

Do **not** expose any NMR at R0. The NMR exists physically and is retained, but it is outside the actual late-stage decision policy.

Also do not expose:

- source experiment IDs;
- target molecule numbers;
- paper figure labels;
- `cyclized`, `unexpected`, `anomaly`, XRD identities;
- high-resolution characterization added after the autonomous workflow;
- filenames or directory paths that encode outcomes.

The public R0 payload is `cooper-r0-blinded-v0.1.json` with SHA-256 `488a40f6b8585e3541cf1896765e3b8c49f2703518a528848de73432bdbf7298`. It contains all ten cohort members; seven have original policy `MS_PASS=true`.

## R1 — retained-data reopening

Reveal the corresponding retained Fourier80 NMR for **all** frozen cohort members through the same opaque IDs. Keep UPLC/MS and all prior R0 evidence available.

The participant may use only a pre-frozen bounded analysis family. Candidate family should include spectral visualization, peak/region inspection, alternative baseline/phase/reference treatments, and comparison against expected/reagent spectra when these are source-fenced inputs. Do not include target-specific hand-written structural templates.

Record separator receipts `(P,X,D,U,R;E)` for each proposal. A valid R1 success may only downgrade/challenge the incumbent intended-product claim; exact alternative structure must not be credited unless R1 evidence genuinely identifies it.

## R2 — orthogonal grounding reveal

Before R2, participant must commit:

1. which opaque sample needs more Reality contact;
2. which claim remains unresolved;
3. requested characterization class;
4. predicted contrast or evidence that would separate current rivals.

Then reveal only a pre-frozen characterization artifact that exists for that sample in the deposited source/CCDC/supplement. No fictional experiment may be supplied.

## Key measurements

- ordinary-control decision reproduction/calibration at R0;
- claim-challenge recall at R1 among original policy-pass cases;
- false R1 challenge rate across ordinary cohort members;
- selective R2 escalation rate rather than `measure everything`;
- epistemic restraint: `incumbent claim challenged` vs `exact alternative identified`;
- whether R2 discrimination updates/grounds the exact claim;
- cost/route burden kept separate from closure bits.
