# Research Significance Map — 2026-08-18/19 Review

## Status

`NEW_CROSS_OWNER_SYNTHESIS` over already-durable owner results.

Purpose: distinguish research that changed Ordivon's world/responsibility model from methodology improvements, architecture formalization, and still-open frontiers. This avoids two opposite errors: treating every result as revolutionary, or flattening genuine conceptual repairs into ordinary documentation.

The classes below describe **kind of impact**, not prestige.

## A — World-model / responsibility-changing results

These materially changed how Ordivon partitions truth or computation:

- Broad Computing decomposition into distinct Meaning / Possibility / Realization / Cognition / Work / Synthesis responsibilities.
- SCD as an independent semantic owner for computational descriptions.
- Computational Possibility as an independent owner for regime-relative possibility/impossibility/reduction/simulation/complexity claims.
- Network `Communicability -> InterLocusCapability` minimal repair under counterexample pressure.
- `OwnerFailure != RealityFailure` as a separation between project/owner admission and ontological status.
- Research Authority / Currentness separated from Git latestness, Host readiness and Atlas projection.
- Agent-first repositioned from universal ontology toward stress/falsification regime.
- Computer repositioned from Broad Computing authority toward cross-owner synthesis, research method and world-model revision.

These are strong candidates for future comparative study because reverting them would materially change the current topology.

## B — Method-changing results

These changed how future research is selected, falsified, repaired, closed or remembered:

- responsibility/owner placement before architecture;
- fresh whole-space search and `Map != Roadmap`;
- prebound destructive falsification;
- typed negative memory;
- minimal repair and non-monotonic research history;
- scoped closure + explicit reopen conditions;
- removal-first / mature classical baseline;
- `ResearchInvestigation != Runtime Run`;
- source-fenced currentness;
- graded reproducibility;
- cross-project convergence does not imply shared service.

These are principally durable in Computer Knowledge / Research System practice rather than new domain ontology.

## C — Architecture-strengthening formalizations

These may be familiar ideas in software/science, but their explicit placement is operationally important in Agent systems:

- `Task != Run != Attempt`;
- `Intent != Effect != SemanticSuccess`;
- `Evidence != Conclusion`;
- `Artifact/Representation != Truth`;
- unqualified `success` is invalid without owner/scope;
- recovery must preserve admitted effect/consequence identity;
- structured control boundary != structured thought process.

Their value is less "new theory" than preventing category errors at real system boundaries.

## D — High-value frontiers, not current admissions

Important questions that should remain open without structure inflation include:

- Phenomenality as a reality/interface question without current independent-owner admission;
- AgentialOrganization as derived/cross-owner rather than a current owner;
- Normative Support as cross-owner relevance rather than automatic Normative ownership;
- richer causal/measurement/temporal tooling only when real consumers fail current local mechanisms;
- SCD semantic-compatibility automation only when repeated consumers earn it;
- CP theorem/applicability automation only when real workload requires more than thin theory/bridge use;
- RSI-style recursive research improvement as a high-potential empirical programme, not an already-proven autonomous self-improvement claim.

## High-potential research directions

The review identified several areas with unusually high long-term leverage:

1. **SCD** — semantic preservation under Agent-generated code/schema/Tool/protocol change.
2. **Computational Possibility** — capability claims under explicit regimes, witnesses, reductions and resource models.
3. **Computer / RSI World Model Loop** — whether research methodology itself measurably improves future question selection, falsification and engineering decisions.
4. **World causality / property evaluation / temporal structure** — especially Agent-generated scores, causal explanations and stale/current observations.
5. **Human–AI joint cognition** — what humans must internalize versus safely externalize/delegate.
6. **Normative semantics** — capability does not imply authority; increasingly important near consequential Agent boundaries.
7. **Network InterLocusCapability** — heterogeneous multi-locus regimes that continue to pressure message-centric network abstractions.

## Current pause / no-numbering-inertia zones

Research maturity also includes knowing when **not** to open the next numbered route. Current consolidation explicitly resists automatic HDF44, HaF62, FDF54, MF10, NDF6, WDF6/TSAF1, HF24/HD17/HOC11, new SCDF, CP Foundation0, ONF/PHR5, etc. Reopen should come from concrete pressure, not sequence completion.

## Research-to-engineering lesson

A major review result is:

`ResearchAdvance != MoreCode`

World's WDF4/WDF5/TSAF0 engineering audit is a canonical example: theory advanced, but current product already preserved the load-bearing causal/measurement/temporal distinctions, so the correct engineering delta was explicit interpretation/reopen boundaries with **no new subsystem**.

SCD/CP similarly gained engineering-consumption boundaries without admitting SemanticCompatibilityService, TheoremTransportService or global registries.

## Caution on originality

This significance map evaluates novelty **inside Ordivon's research/world-model evolution**. It does not claim that individual ideas are unprecedented in the wider academic literature. Many have deep precedents in philosophy, semantics, theoretical computer science, distributed systems, measurement and scientific methodology. The Ordivon-specific contribution lies substantially in their cross-domain recombination, owner placement, falsification history and engineering consequences under Agent-first pressure.
