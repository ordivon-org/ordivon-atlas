## Research lifecycle

A useful default sequence is:

`Problem -> Owner Placement -> Whole-Space Search -> Candidate/Rival -> Prebound Falsifier -> Investigation -> Evidence -> Evaluation -> Minimal Residual -> Admission/Repair -> Scoped Closure -> Engineering Consumption -> Cross-Owner Synthesis -> Reopen`

This is **not a compulsory workflow**. It is a map of research responsibilities.

## Stable-ish epistemic invariants

The current review suggests a small set of invariants that should survive changes in tooling/templates:

1. **Question before mechanism** — know what proposition is being investigated before designing machinery.
2. **Owner before ontology** — identify the truth-condition responsibility before adding a Foundation, owner, service, or schema.
3. **Evidence scope must be explicit** — a successful run, source, benchmark, or argument proves only what its contract supports.
4. **Result standing must be explicit** — distinguish admitted, falsified, underdetermined, repaired, reduced, not-admitted, and historical-only results.
5. **Currentness/provenance must be explicit** — historical validity is not currentness; source advancement can stale a conclusion without falsifying its historical validity.
6. **Recovery cannot mint authority** — restored work/history does not become current truth merely because it is recoverable.
7. **Closure is scoped and reopenable** — local closure is not whole-domain exhaustion.

## High-value strategies, not constitutional steps

These are currently effective but should remain removable/falsifiable methods rather than bureaucracy:

- fresh whole-space search;
- rival construction;
- destructive falsification;
- prebound oracle/falsifier;
- minimal repair;
- removal-first / mature-baseline comparison;
- fresh synthesis context;
- ablation and independent-consumer testing.

## Minimal repair pattern

When a load-bearing claim fails:

`counterexample -> identify hidden assumption -> preserve surviving structure -> repair minimal dependency cone -> retain history -> re-freeze only in the repaired scope`.

Network's Communicability -> InterLocusCapability genealogy is the canonical current case.

## Negative memory

Do not collapse all non-positive outcomes into `FAILED`.

At minimum distinguish when relevant:

- `FALSIFIED`
- `UNDERDETERMINED`
- `NOT_ADMITTED`
- `BASELINE_EQUIVALENT`
- `NO_DETECTABLE_EFFECT`
- `REPAIR_REQUIRED`
- `EXECUTION_INVALID`
- `NO_CHANGE_REQUIRED`

Typed negatives are research capital because they determine different future actions and reduce repeated dead-end work.

## Research -> Engineering

Research does not imply feature creation. Legitimate engineering deltas include:

- `NO_CHANGE`
- boundary/documentation repair
- contract narrowing
- implementation change
- implementation deletion
- new capability earned by repeated evidence
- further experiment required

Theory can advance while engineering correctly adds no subsystem.

## Research System boundary

Structure what is needed for semantic recovery and control:

- Question/work identity
- owner
- source/evidence provenance
- evaluation/standing
- currentness
- closeout/reopen

Do **not** require every thought, hypothesis turn, or reasoning step to become a schema object. Structure control boundaries, not the thought process.
