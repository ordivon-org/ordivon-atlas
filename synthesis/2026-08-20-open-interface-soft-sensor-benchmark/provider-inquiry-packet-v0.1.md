# Stage-A/B Physical Authority Inquiry Packet v0.1

Status: non-authoritative acquisition-control artifact. This packet does not alter the frozen benchmark, `WP-I C0`, `WP-G G0`, thresholds, or receipt semantics.

Purpose: qualify a commissioned mechanical-test provider and, where needed, a separate printed-electronics fabricator for **target-preserving execution** of the already-frozen Open-Interface soft-sensor benchmark. Provider convenience or standard-method substitutions must not redefine the scientific target.

## Response classes

A provider route is `EXTERNAL_EXECUTABLE` only if every HARD requirement relevant to its assigned role is answered YES in writing and can be bound into the test/quote/protocol.

- `EXTERNAL_EXECUTABLE`: all assigned HARD gates accepted.
- `PARTIAL_R5`: useful concept-to-part or ordinary mechanical-testing authority, but one or more Stage-A/B semantic gates missing; may support weaker physical reachability only.
- `NOT_TARGET_PRESERVING`: provider requires a protocol change that collapses G0/G1 staging, fresh replicas, hidden-label firewall, or post-evidence morphology provenance.
- `UNKNOWN`: marketing/public capability only; no written project-specific commitment.

No scalar vendor score substitutes for these gates.

## Scientific boundary that may not change

The test contains two mirror-related physical conditions at matched deformation magnitude. Stage A exposes only a frozen mirror-even measurement grammar (`G0`) and must demonstrate old-interface blindness within owner-calibrated tolerance. Only **after Stage-A evidence is frozen** may a new asymmetric sensing morphology/operator be selected, source/geometry hashed, fabricated/installed, and evaluated in Stage B on untouched replicas. The successful claim is only bounded closure escape relative to the frozen `G0`; it is not a claim of ultimate autonomous physical invention.

## A. Mechanical-test provider — HARD yes/no gates

Please answer each item YES / NO / CONDITIONAL and identify the exact lab/equipment/workflow.

### A1. Custom specimen and fixture

**HARD** — Can you test a customer-defined symmetric deformable/elastomeric cartridge or soft component in a custom fixture rather than only standardized coupons?

**HARD** — Can the fixture/loading arrangement implement mirror-related positive/negative or left/right bending/loading states at matched deformation/load magnitude?

**HARD** — Can the same protocol be repeated across multiple physically independent cartridges/replicas held out from exploratory work?

### A2. Controlled actuation and timing

**HARD** — Can you execute programmed cyclic or repeated loading with customer-defined magnitude, rate/frequency, duration, dwell and ordering?

**HARD** — Can you preserve stable execution IDs and timestamps across all raw channels and stages?

### A3. Frozen Stage-A `G0` instrumentation

**HARD** — Can Stage A be run with only the customer-specified old measurement set, without adding an asymmetric/labeled strain channel to the scientific output?

Required old channels may include total force/load/energy and mirror-even aggregate resistance/strain responses. Owner/calibration-only channels may exist internally but must not leak into the scientific-policy export.

**HARD** — Can you return raw time-series data (not only a summary report) together with units, sample rates, calibration records and channel identities?

### A4. Hidden-label / metadata firewall

**HARD** — Can physical condition labels be supplied/maintained as owner-coded IDs such that the agent/scientific-analysis export does not reveal target sign through filenames, operator notes, channel names, actuator commands or report metadata?

The lab may know what it physically executes; the requirement is that target truth remains in an independent adjudication path and is not leaked into the scientific-policy evidence packet.

### A5. Two-stage pause / evidence-dependent resume

**HARD** — Can the project explicitly pause after Stage A, with Stage-A files/checksums frozen, and later resume without redesigning Stage A?

**HARD** — At resume, can you accept a **new customer-provided, hash-frozen asymmetric sensing geometry/configuration that was not selected at project start**, install/fabricate it or accept a fresh cartridge carrying it, and then run Stage B under the original target/loading protocol?

A preselected finite menu of directional sensors is not equivalent to this requirement.

### A6. Fresh holdout and nuisance control

**HARD** — Can specified cartridges/replicas remain untouched until final blinded Stage-B validation?

**HARD** — Can remount/contact/cable/preload/order nuisance conditions be randomized or executed from an owner-provided schedule, with the realized schedule recorded?

**HARD** — Can the new sensor be evaluated under target reversal and nuisance changes separately, rather than only under pooled trials?

### A7. Provenance

**HARD** — Can the final package include specimen IDs, fixture/config revision, sensor geometry/source digest, fabrication/installation record, material/process lot where applicable, calibration records, raw channel files, execution log and deviations/failures?

### A8. Later replay (may be separate quote)

**DESIRED, not required for base Stage B** — Can a later context-reset run be performed on fresh mirror-equivalent bodies using a frozen validated sensor/operator, with matched registry/no-registry conditions supplied by the owner?

**OPTIONAL** — Can a shifted body/actuation family be tested later for transport/revalidation?

## B. Printed-electronics / DIW fabricator — HARD yes/no gates

This section applies when the mechanical-test provider cannot itself materialize the post-evidence morphology.

### B1. Customer-defined post-evidence geometry

**HARD** — Can you fabricate a one-off or small batch conductive sensing path from a customer-supplied drawing/vector/path/geometry after the project has already begun, without requiring the final geometry to be fixed at initial quotation?

**HARD** — Can conductive/resistive ink be printed/deposited on the supplied flexible/deformable substrate or on a target-compatible fresh cartridge, terminating on specified landing pads?

### B2. Source and process provenance

**HARD** — Can the fabrication record bind the customer geometry/source digest to the produced parts and report material/ink identity or lot, substrate, print process, curing/sintering/postprocess and relevant process revision?

**HARD** — Can multiple fresh nominally matched replicas be produced from the same frozen geometry after candidate selection?

### B3. Electrical sanity receipt

**HARD** — Can you provide or permit pre-blind electrical continuity/resistance sanity tests without mechanically pre-exposing the heldout cartridges to the target validation sequence?

### B4. No semantic redesign

**HARD** — Will you manufacture the frozen customer morphology as specified (subject to explicit manufacturability/safety rejection) rather than silently optimizing the sensing geometry based on target labels or Stage-B outcomes?

Any required geometry change receives a new identity/hash and is not treated as the same candidate.

## C. Hybrid handoff — HARD gates

If mechanical test and fabrication are split between organizations:

**HARD** — Can fresh cartridges be reserved for Stage B before Stage-A analysis?

**HARD** — Can the post-evidence geometry digest, cartridge ID and fabrication provenance survive transfer between the fabricator and test lab?

**HARD** — Can Stage-B fixtures/loading/calibration remain traceably equivalent to Stage A except for the admitted new sensing operator and preregistered nuisance changes?

**HARD** — Can both providers return sufficient raw/provenance records to reconstruct the chain without relying on an informal technician recollection?

## D. Quote dimensions — report separately, do not scalarize

Please provide separately:

- one-time engineering / custom-fixture cost;
- per-specimen / per-replica cost;
- instrumentation / DAQ / strain-gauge installation cost;
- pause/resume or change-order constraints;
- minimum project/order size;
- estimated lead time for Stage A and post-evidence Stage B;
- sample shipping / customs / storage requirements;
- raw-data deliverables and format;
- accreditation status for the exact custom method, if any;
- confidentiality/IP constraints for customer-generated sensor geometry;
- whether the lab can preserve coded labels and restricted metadata exports.

Evidence quality, cost, latency, and authority are separate comparison dimensions.

## E. Hard-fail interpretation

The route is **not** a target-preserving Stage-A/B substitute if any of the following is required:

1. selecting the final asymmetric sensor before Stage-A evidence;
2. exposing directional/target-sign metadata in the Stage-A scientific packet;
3. replacing fresh physical replicas with repeated trials on one explored body;
4. refusing raw/calibration/provenance data needed for independent adjudication;
5. changing the old `G0` measurement definition after Stage-A outcomes;
6. disallowing a pause/resume or evidence-dependent new morphology;
7. provider optimization of the candidate using hidden target labels after candidate hash freeze.

Such a route may still be useful as `PARTIAL_R5` physical reachability evidence, but it cannot close the frozen prospective benchmark.

## F. Standing preserved by external execution

A commissioned or hybrid route can, if all gates pass, establish bounded external-executor receipts for physical old-interface calibration, post-evidence materialization, blinded discrimination, nuisance grounding and independent-replica quotient break. It **does not** by itself establish strict autonomous physical materialization or ultimate missing-primitive/meta-closure escape.
