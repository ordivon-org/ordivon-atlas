# Mechanism Observations

These are **observed explanatory mechanisms**, not required workflow stages and not claims of universal methodological superiority.

| Ref | Mechanism | Bounded observation | Support profile | Known limit / reopen pressure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MO-M01` | Permissive Exploration | Weak/UNKNOWN project, Foundation or product standing can coexist with bounded search, rival construction and falsification when the investigation's own consequence authority is satisfied. | `CROSS_OWNER_RECURRENT_QUALITATIVE` | Exploration itself can be high-consequence; ethical/external/resource authority is independent. |
| `MO-M02` | Problem / Representation Challenge | Question identity, candidate basis, evaluator/search space and owner decomposition can themselves be wrong and can be revised after evidence. | `RECURRENT_QUALITATIVE` | Most work occurs under stable frames; reframing is a minority mode, not a constant requirement. |
| `MO-M03` | Falsification + Typed Negative Knowledge | Failed branches, falsifiers, absorbed novelty claims and superseded mechanisms can retain durable why-not/provenance/reopen value. | `CROSS_OWNER_STRONG_QUALITATIVE` | Preserved but undiscoverable material still causes rediscovery; raw bytes may be inadmissible to retain. |
| `MO-M04` | Typed Standing + Promotion | Usefulness, ownerhood, Foundation status, route currentness, novelty, engineering need and effect authority repeatedly diverge; content can survive at lower standing after higher-standing failure. | `CROSS_OWNER_STRONG_QUALITATIVE` | Standing can itself be stale, wrong, undiscoverable or overbroad. |
| `MO-M05` | Standing-Mediated Commitment (SMC) | Stronger semantic/engineering/effect commitments are often withheld until contemporaneous standing supports them, while weaker exploration continues. | `MEDIUM_HIGH_QUALITATIVE_NOT_STATISTICAL` | Does not prevent implementation bugs; explicit standing can overauthorize; weak boundaries may produce no observed harm by luck/recoverability. |
| `MO-M06` | Human–Agent Joint Judgment | Human steering is load-bearing in sequence/intent corrections, but Human-Agent agreement is not truth and can itself overcommit. | `STRONG_QUALITATIVE` | Actor contribution shares are difficult to reconstruct without private reasoning; avoid false attribution. |
| `MO-M07` | Durable Memory / Currentness / Recovery Separation | Historical survival, current authority, recovery ability and live capability are distinct; continuity can remain true after semantic currentness has moved. | `STRONG_QUALITATIVE` | Owner-unknown discovery remains incomplete; zero-trace loss is censored. |
| `MO-M08` | Engineering / Effect Consumption Boundary | Supported research does not automatically authorize refactor, deployment, trade, deletion or other external effects; NO_CHANGE is a valid consumption outcome. | `CROSS_OWNER_STRONG_QUALITATIVE` | Overly narrow gates can suppress fragile options; cheap reversible prototyping still needs room. |
| `MO-M09` | Verification + Recoverability | Mechanical mutation errors are a distinct failure family; tests, exact state fences, receipts, reconciliation and snapshots reduce repair uncertainty. | `STRONG_QUALITATIVE` | Recoverability is not universally desirable; revoked secret capability may need to remain unrecoverable while lineage survives. |
| `MO-M10` | Survivorship + Workload Selection | Memorable surviving correction stories overstate prevalence; balanced/fixed-window sampling shows stable-frame work dominates and zero-trace loss is underobservable. | `METHODOLOGICAL_CAVEAT_STRONG` | Selection bias does not explain every within-episode demotion/currentness effect. |

## Stable cross-mechanism observations

```text
Exploration breadth != promotion authority
Evidence != result != usefulness != currentness != ownerhood != engineering/effect authority
Negative != useless
Historical continuity != semantic currentness
Mechanical correctness != semantic standing
No-op / defer / demote / derived-only can be progress
```

## Evidence and prescription boundary

The original observational corpus sometimes records a practical implication next to an observation. In this Atlas M0 projection, the **mechanism observation** is retained here; practice prescriptions remain owned by Practice Guidance / Operating Discipline and are referenced, not copied as mechanism truth.
