# Coverage and Bias Manifest

Methodology Observation is built from Ordivon's surviving research history. Coverage limits are therefore first-class content, not footnotes.

## Current coverage profile

| Dimension | Current observation |
|---|---|
| Owner/domain breadth | Multiple independent owners and cross-owner programmes are represented, with especially dense Game, Finance, Computing, Network/Interlocus, Runtime/Harness and Research-System evidence. Coverage is not uniform. |
| Prospective vs retrospective | Both exist. Later evidence increasingly includes prospective boundary/dogfood cases; much early material remains retrospective reconstruction. |
| Fixed-window vs selected cases | Game and Finance fixed-window/trajectory audits exist and materially weakened a dramatic self-correction narrative. Many other examples are selected high-information cases. |
| Positive vs negative evidence | Both are present, but failure/repair episodes are more memorable and more likely to receive durable documentation. |
| Destructive/high-consequence representation | Overrepresented because these episodes force explicit authority/currentness/recovery reasoning and are heavily documented. |
| Stable-frame ordinary research | Common in reality but less narratively salient; likely underrepresented in curated episode collections. |
| Zero-trace loss | Fundamentally censored: material that left no recoverable trace cannot enter the observed corpus. |
| Human/Agent attribution | Often underdetermined because private reasoning is intentionally not archived; joint outcomes should not be decomposed into unsupported contribution percentages. |
| External/general-population validity | Not established. This is a longitudinal Ordivon internal ecology, not a controlled comparative methodology trial. |

## Selection-bias law

```text
SurvivingDocumentedEpisode != UnbiasedSampleOfResearch
MemorableRepair != PrevalenceEstimate
CrossOwnerReplication != StatisticalLaw
```

`MO-M10` exists specifically to prevent the rest of this collection from being misread as a quality/safety rate claim.

## Evidence-strength interpretation

Support profiles in `MECHANISMS.md` are qualitative descriptors of the current corpus shape. They are not probabilities, p-values, confidence scores or scalar standing.

- `STRONG_QUALITATIVE` means repeated load-bearing observation with meaningful contrasts/counterexamples.
- `CROSS_OWNER_*` means analogous structure appears in more than one owner/context.
- `MEDIUM_HIGH_QUALITATIVE_NOT_STATISTICAL` explicitly marks SMC as a plausible recurrent mechanism that is not established as a statistical law.
- `METHODOLOGICAL_CAVEAT_STRONG` means the caveat itself is strongly evidenced and constrains interpretation of other observations.

## Reopen pressures

Revisit this manifest when:

1. a new fixed-window sample materially changes the apparent prevalence of a mechanism/failure;
2. a previously undercovered owner exhibits a stable counter-pattern;
3. low-consequence ordinary work contradicts conclusions drawn from destructive/high-consequence cases;
4. autonomous discovery exposes substantial zero-trace-like omission among material documented episodes;
5. a controlled comparison with a simpler workflow becomes available.

Do not compensate for bias by inventing a global methodology-quality score.
