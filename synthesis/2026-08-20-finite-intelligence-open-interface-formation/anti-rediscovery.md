# Anti-Rediscovery Guard

## Read this before reopening the thread

Do **not** restart from any of the following already-established questions as if they were the live frontier:

- whether more data inside a fixed observation/model basis solves open-world blindness — it does not in general;
- whether current uncertainty/confidence measures Reality uncertainty/model-class adequacy — they do not;
- whether unknown-unknown detection can be guaranteed under a fixed interface — not without assumptions/support;
- whether all-green monitoring proves adequacy — no;
- whether random audits/random exploration create missing semantics — no; they only avoid some zero-support lock-in;
- whether incommensurable bases are therefore empirically incomparable — no; local bridges can permit partial comparison;
- whether a new basis must first beat the incumbent on incumbent metrics — no; TruthAdmission and PursuitAdmission are distinct;
- whether standard OED/bandits already solve open experiment-space generation — no; they solve strong inner fixed-support problems;
- whether every anomaly should trigger theory change — no; preserve validated-but-unassimilated evidence and diagnose repair trajectory;
- whether many/large anomalies alone imply basis failure — no; repair productivity, transport, independent constraints and challengeability matter more;
- whether a plateau proves a research programme exhausted — no; repair-operator/capability/exposure deficits can mimic exhaustion;
- whether generating many questions means open problem space — no;
- whether current utility/novelty/surprise/salience can universally determine importance — no;
- whether relevance ranking solves candidate formation — no;
- whether Reality has one unique observation-only latent factorization available without inductive assumptions — no;
- whether finer distinctions are always better — no; false split and false merge are symmetric risks;
- whether invariance or predictive equivalence proves ontological identity — no;
- whether automatic symmetry discovery solves unrestricted transformation-language invention — no;
- whether semantic similarity / nearest-neighbor retrieval solves deep comparability — no;
- whether a perfect deep mapper solves discovery if candidate gating misses the pair — no.
