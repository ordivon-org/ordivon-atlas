## Why this synthesis exists

This research thread began from a narrow pressure: a finite intelligence should not merely wait for Reality to supply natural contradictions; it can actively alter the ways Reality can exert discriminating pressure. The investigation then expanded far beyond proactive falsification. Across twenty-six conversational rounds it repeatedly found that the upstream conditions for discovery are themselves revisable: model space, experiment space, observation basis, question language, relevance criteria, candidate individuation, identity relations, transformation languages and comparability neighborhoods can all be incomplete.

The durable result is **not** one new grand theory. It is an open problem lineage with a growing set of scoped non-equivalences, impossibility boundaries and developmental escape routes. Existing disciplines provide powerful local tools but none is admitted here as the final ontology.

## Root standing

Preserve:

- `Reality != Representation`.
- `PosteriorUncertainty != RealityUncertainty`.
- `UncertaintyReduction != BlindnessReduction`.
- `CurrentModelConfidence != ModelClassAdequacy`.
- `MoreData != NewObservationDimension`.
- `MoreObservations != NewInterventionCapability`.
- `MoreHypothesisDiscrimination != HypothesisBasisCompleteness`.
- current support may need to expand: knowledge progress is not always hypothesis-space shrinkage.

Reality difference becomes actionable pressure only through a chain such as activation -> manifestation -> transduction -> sampling -> representation -> contrast -> discrepancy -> attribution -> revision. A finite interface can fail at every link. If two Reality states induce the same laws over all currently reachable contacts, no current-interface procedure can discriminate them. This is an interface-relative impossibility, not a proof of ontological identity.

## Research trajectory retained from this conversation

### 1. Active Reality-pressure acquisition

External papers, existing experiments, historical failed theories, interventions, new sensors, query changes, representation forks, adversarial countermodels and first-principles constructions can do more than provide citations: they can import or generate new candidate variables, breaker tests, intervention families and structural alternatives.

But fixed support remains a ceiling. `Expressible != Findable != Recognizable != SemanticallyGrounded != PhysicallyActionable`.

### 2. Basis challenge without local anomaly

All-green monitoring cannot generate a failure-specific trigger for a failure observationally invisible to the monitor basis. Therefore `NoObservedPressure != NoRealityStructure`. Bounded heterogeneous challenge may require periodic/random audits, external witnesses, shadow bases, sensor/representation perturbation or support-expansion probes. Randomization prevents permanent zero sampling support inside an eligible generator; it does not create missing semantics.

### 3. Cross-basis admission and Reality arbitration

`Incommensurability != Incomparability`. Full neutral translation is not necessary when rival bases can form a local operational bridge through shared consequences, actions, anchors or downstream decisions. Yet `BridgeAvailability != BridgeValidity` and `OperationalBridge != TheoryNeutralBridge`.

Separate inheritance from expansion: `BackwardCompatibility != ForwardEpistemicValue`. Separate truth from pursuit: a new basis can deserve bounded Reality access without being believed. `Admission != GlobalDominance != Replacement`.

### 4. Who buys the next Reality contact?

Standard model-discrimination OED, Bayesian design and bandits solve strong inner problems once candidate model/action/reward spaces are fixed. They do not solve open experiment-generator formation. `OptimalExperimentUnderB != OptimalExperimentForChallengingB`.

Finite budget plus unbounded candidate space forbids universal coverage. Challenger access must avoid incumbent-defined zero support, but no finite system can give every conceivable basis a uniform positive exploration floor. Research resources also buy future capability: sensors, instruments, skills, bridges and experiment generators. `ImmediateEvidenceValue != FutureInterfaceExpansionValue`.

### 5. Cross-basis evidence uptake

Evidence need not immediately become theory. Preserve:

- `ObservationStanding != InterpretationStanding != TheoryRevisionStanding`;
- validated-but-unassimilated evidence is a legitimate state;
- `AnomalyExplanation != AnomalyDischarge`;
- `BetterAccommodation != BetterExplanation`;
- `RobustInference != AnomalyDisposal`.

A mature basis can become an anti-discovery mechanism when its auxiliary flexibility absorbs every discrepancy without generating new Reality-constrained consequences.

### 6. Local anomaly -> basis-level pressure

Anomaly magnitude alone is weak. Stronger pressure appears in **repair-trajectory morphology**: nontransporting local fixes, complexity growth without independent new constraints, structured residuals, parameter compensation, joint-accommodation failure, scope retreat, repair externalities and repairs that reduce future challengeability.

`BasisPressure != BasisReplacementJustification`. Escalation may change research allocation while the incumbent remains operationally useful.

### 7. Repair potential vs basis-switch option value

`CurrentPerformance != FutureRepairPotential`. A plateau may mean true exhaustion, current repair-operator exhaustion, missing prerequisite capability, insufficient Reality exposure or evaluation blindness. No finite repair history certifies that an unrestricted programme has no future breakthrough path.

Repair potential can nevertheless be probed actively: change repair operators, capabilities and regimes. `RepairTrajectoryDegeneration != ProgrammeDegeneration`. Continue/abandon is too binary: local repair, repair-process refactor, imported methods, alternative-basis incubation, bridge building and partial migration are distinct possibilities.

### 8. Problem-space generativity

`ProblemSolving != ProblemFinding != QuestionGeneration != ProblemSpaceExpansion`. Infinite questions inside a fixed language can remain closed. New problem families can arise from concept split/merge, relation formation, new sensors, interventions, scales, impossibility results and new units of analysis. `NoAnomaly != NoNewProblem`.

A programme can solve many known problems while narrowing what can become a problem. Conversely, a tool or representation can have low immediate answer yield while expanding the reachable future question space.

### 9. Relevance formation

Reality contains far more distinguishable structure than finite attention can pursue. Relevance is not a context-free scalar property of a distinction; it is relational to agent capability, goals, environment and future possibilities. Current utility, surprise, novelty, salience, causal effect magnitude, compression, learning progress and empowerment are useful partial cues but none is a universal importance oracle.

Preserve `FutureImportancePrediction != FutureImportanceRecoverability`. When deletion is hard to reverse and retention is cheap, `LowEstimatedImportance != SafeToErase`. Also keep `Preserve != Attend != Pursue`.

### 10. Candidate distinction / foreground object formation

Before relevance ranking, Reality variation must first become a candidate. Candidate formation is itself an inductive, lossy hypothesis about sameness and difference; observational data alone does not determine a unique latent factorization without additional structure.

`MaximumResolution != MaximumEpistemicPower`: zero compression destroys abstraction while aggressive compression can destroy future distinctions. Candidate decompositions should therefore be provisional and experimentally splittable/mergeable. `CandidateIndividuation != OntologicalClassification`; phenomenon-level standing can precede ontology.

### 11. Identity / invariance formation

Sameness is scoped. Preserve:

- `Indistinguishability != Identity`;
- `RepresentationalDifference != RealityDifference`;
- `MeasurementInvariance != ConstructIdentity`;
- `BehavioralEquivalence != OntologicalIdentity`;
- `LocalSameness != GlobalSameness`.

False merge and false split are symmetric risks. Identity can drift through locally small changes; pairwise correspondences can fail global composition. Replication itself can test identity assumptions by varying dimensions treated as irrelevant and observing whether a phenomenon survives.

### 12. Transformation formation

`TransformationTesting != TransformationDiscovery != TransformationLanguageFormation`. Automatic symmetry discovery is a major local advance but still searches inside a supplied transformation meta-family. General transformation formation also includes noninvertible coarse-graining, representation maps, intervention mappings, query transforms, concept splits and stochastic transductions.

Representation, candidates and transformation languages co-develop. A new representation can make a previously complex transformation simple; a discovered transformation can create new invariants and candidates. `TransformationSearchScale != TransformationLanguageEscape`.

### 13. Comparability formation

Comparison is costly and therefore gated. A perfect deep mapper cannot discover an analogy that a cheap candidate gate never presents. Preserve:

- `ComparisonQuality != ComparisonOpportunityQuality`;
- `StructuralValue != StructuralRetrievability`;
- `InformationStored != InformationComparablyRetrievable`;
- `SemanticRetrieval != StructuralOpportunityRetrieval`;
- `RetrieverDiversity != ComparabilityBasisDiversity`.

Comparability is lens-relative: surface, relational, causal, dynamical, functional, anomaly-morphology, intervention-response and repair-history signatures generate different neighbors. Successful comparison can create a relational schema that becomes a new retrieval coordinate; therefore `CurrentlyIncomparable != DevelopmentallyIncomparable`.

## Repeated cross-layer pattern

Several layers show the same scoped structure without proving they are instances of one ontology:

1. a finite system must compress/select;
2. the selector/generator has support and common-mode ceilings;
3. current optimization inside support can be excellent while support itself is incomplete;
4. permanent zero support produces evidence starvation;
5. random/exogenous challenge can break deterministic lock-in but not invent absent semantics;
6. tools, representations, interventions and intermediate abstractions can expand developmental reachability;
7. closure should therefore be scoped and recoverable rather than treated as global completeness.

This pattern is a cross-round observation, **not** an admitted unified theory.

## Hard impossibility / no-free-lunch boundaries retained

- Finite current contact cannot distinguish Reality states that induce identical histories over all reachable probes.
- Fixed candidate/model/question/experiment/transformation generators cannot select elements outside their support.
- Infinite support from a fixed generator is still not unrestricted generative support.
- Finite budget + unbounded candidate space forbids universal positive allocation to every possible basis, experiment, problem or comparison.
- No context-free relevance ranking can be guaranteed correct for unrestricted future tasks, capabilities and values.
- No finite observational history can certify permanent global exhaustion of an unrestricted research programme.
- No finite lossy candidate-gating representation can guarantee recall of every future deep correspondence.
- No finite present basis can certify global open-world completeness.

These boundaries imply `NoGuarantee != NoSystematicImprovement`.
