# Anti-Rediscovery Guard — RSI/PAL Option, Pressure, and Capability Frontier

## Purpose

Prevent future conversations from re-opening already-subtracted theory claims or promoting current capability gaps into architecture by vocabulary alone.

## Do not rediscover as new theory

- `OptionFormation != Selection` by itself.
- consideration-set formation / limited consideration.
- unawareness, missing moves/states/actions, awareness growth.
- representation-dependent search or planning-language expressivity.
- problem finding/formulation versus problem solving.
- agenda/order/framing/attention effects on choice.
- perturbation/counterexample refinement as a generic method.
- external challenge/contestability as a generic safeguard.
- aspiration/gap-triggered search.
- opportunity recognition/discovery/creation.
- worthwhile-problem versus currently feasible experiment.
- experiment/portfolio allocation after the question/project set is supplied.

These are mature prior-art regions. Ordivon may consume them, test them, or synthesize them without pretending they are blank-slate discoveries.

- candidate count;

- recursive depth;
- persistence volume;
- current option count;
- short-run performance alone;

Owner evidence already contains direct counterexamples to several of these proxies.
