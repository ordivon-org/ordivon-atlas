## Why a vector, not a score

Current Ordivon evidence falsifies several monotonic proxies for intelligence/improvement. More candidates, more search/evidence, more context, deeper recursion, or more persistent state are not uniformly beneficial. Capability therefore needs typed standing.

The labels below are qualitative evidence dispositions, not a leaderboard or protocol enum.

## Evidence details

### 1. Mandate/value anchors

Strong evidence here means preservation and typed authority, not autonomous value creation. A pressure-formulation Agent may reason relative to a mandate; it does not thereby earn power to replace that mandate.

### 2. Reality/currentness

Computing P2 moved beyond syntax-only revision checking into relation/currentness reasoning across a different evidence family. This is one of the clearer cross-family RSI positives.

### 3. Problem formation

This is the clearest current **negative capability** region. C6 directly falsified the then-current generic selector representation. F17 tests a materially different prospective representation/oracle and must not be prejudged.

### 4. Probe formation

Ordivon is much better at designing a discriminating experiment once the problem is bounded than at autonomously deciding which problem deserves attention. This asymmetry should remain visible.

### 5. Candidate generation

Current evidence rejects `variation is the bottleneck by default`. Better coverage may matter in some cases, but extra candidates can create downstream selection/validity cost and do not earn infrastructure by themselves.

### 6. Selection

`CandidatePresent != CandidateSelected`. This is a distinct cognitive frontier. Strong governance around selection does not imply selector optimality.

### 7–8. Execution and Reality return

These are among Ordivon's strongest regions. Their strength comes partly from refusing semantic overreach: Runtime does not own domain outcome, receipts do not own truth, and uncertainty is not collapsed into retry permission.

### 9. Capitalization / transfer

Evidence is stronger than an earlier PAL R0 snapshot implied:

- F18: one natural support-bound revalidation positive;
- F19: one real invariant-bound Harness migration positive;
- Web: an earlier rendered-pixel inspection prior was consumed on materially different `/about`/`/colophon` surfaces, exposed a real desktop residual, and produced a localized full-gate-passing correction while controls stayed byte-identical;
- Media: materially different Writing, social-card and motion Productions repeatedly reuse/reinforce rendered-artifact inspection and exact-byte durability principles while refusing universal visual scores or universal CAS/R2.

This still does **not** prove human preference/comprehension transfer or broad cross-domain RSI capitalization.

### 10. Self-change governance

Bounded self-reform is already demonstrated. What remains unidentifiable is the marginal value of regulation against an endogenous loop in which a change can manipulate the evidence/evaluator that selects descendants. Current safeguards should not be removed merely to manufacture that case.

## Three deficit classes

### NEGATIVE CAPABILITY

A mechanism was directly tested and failed.

Example: generic autonomous pressure/problem selection under C6.

Next action: require materially different evidence/representation, not cosmetic retry.

### PARTIAL / TRANSFER-EVIDENCE GAP

Scoped positives exist but generalization, second independent family, or longitudinal consumption remains insufficient.

Examples: broad retention/capitalization; parts of candidate generation and selection.

Next action: wait for/reuse natural transfer cases and independent consumers.

### NON-IDENTIFIABLE MARGINAL CONTROL

The causal concern is plausible but current reality lacks a clean treatment/control contrast.

Example: F16 endogenous self-reinforcement regulation.

Next action: wait for a natural identifiable case; do not treat non-identifiability as falsification or inject a synthetic failure merely to justify machinery.
