# CP Core Reconstruction — From Achievability Predicate to Possibility Structure

## 1. Starting problem

The already-formed CP project had a clean canonical question:

> Under an explicitly declared computational situation, does an admissible computational witness exist that satisfies the constituted obligation and declared constraints?

The destructive re-reading asked whether this wording accidentally made CP look like a theory of one Boolean predicate or one eight-coordinate tuple.

## 2. What survived

For an already constituted situation `C`:

`Achievable(C) iff exists w in Adm(C) such that w satisfies the complete constituted obligation/resource contract.`

This existential engine survived, but only with an important qualification:

> the outer existential is a **guarded strategy normal form**.

It may package a strategy/witness, but theorem-bearing quantifier order, role polarity, uniformity, visibility, probability scope and causal dependency must remain explicit/recoverable.

Therefore:

- `forall x exists y` is not silently `exists M forall x`;
- alternating/adversarial choices may be strategy-packaged only with dependency restrictions preserved;
- provider behavior, stochastic law and interaction history may not be hidden inside an opaque `Sat` predicate when they change computational truth.

## 3. Claim formation precedes possibility

A key repair was to separate formation failure from computational falsehood.

Useful judgment:

`C : ConstitutedComputationalSituation`.

Only then is `Achievable(C)` a well-formed proposition, with:

`Impossible(C) := not Achievable(C)`.

Permanent controls:

- `Underconstituted != Impossible`;
- `NoWitnessFound != NoWitnessExists`;
- `Unknown/Open != third computational truth value`.

The eight canonical coordinates remain current coverage/responsibility projections, not mandatory fields, proven orthogonal dimensions or a primitive ontology.

## 4. Three levels, not one central noun

The false trilemma between situation, capability and possibility structure was resolved by level separation.

### Claim / typing unit

`ComputationalSituation`

It constitutes what problem is being asked and which information, role, interaction, resource and semantic conditions are load-bearing.

### Witness / action unit

`Admissible computational agency/capability`

Programs, strategies, protocols, samplers, controllers, families, trajectories, higher-order capability transforms and other theorem-appropriate witnesses inhabit a situation only relative to its admissibility rules.

### Theory / comparison unit

`Computational Possibility Structure`

A useful explanatory shorthand is:

`Poss(C)`

for the theorem-appropriate local successful/admissible witness-capability structure plus resource/frontier enrichment when relevant.

`Achievable(C)` is only the non-emptiness projection.

The global theory studies local `Poss(C)` and typed relations between possibility structures as situations/capabilities/representations/resources change.

## 5. Why CP is independent from nearby owners

### SCD

SCD owns meaning, representation, semantic comparison/refinement and semantic preservation. CP consumes those semantics and asks what computational agency is admissible/possible under additional computational conditions.

Permanent seam:

`SemanticEquivalence != ComputationalEquivalence`.

### Runtime

Runtime owns actual Jobs, Attempts, execution, lifecycle and resource evidence. CP owns quantified possibility claims.

`OneSuccessfulRun != UniformFamilyAchievability`.

### Interlocus

Interlocus owns actual cross-locus reachability/topology/routing/communication capability. CP may study abstract communication-constrained possibility.

`ActualCommunicationCapability != AbstractCommunicationConstrainedPossibility`.

### Harness

Harness owns concrete Agent/tool/provider mediation and Run admission. CP may analyze formal capability substitution/composition relative to a consumer contract.

`ToolProvision != CapabilitySemantics`.

### Normative / Game / source owners

Normative owns may/should/required/forbidden truth; Game/Mechanism/domain owners own payoff/strategy/domain semantics. CP may ask whether the source-owned constraints leave a computationally realizable witness space, without deciding those source semantics.

## 6. Current owner identity

A compact explanatory formulation:

> **CP studies how constituted computational situations determine possibility structures and how those structures vary, refine, compose and transport across regimes, resources, representations and capabilities.**

This explains the zero-Foundation result: CP's theoretical density lies mostly in typing, quantification, admissibility, interaction, composition, refinement and transport rather than a large primitive noun inventory.
