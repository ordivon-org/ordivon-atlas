`C -> Poss(C)`

## Four research-product families

The positive synthesis contracted many candidate products into four first-class families:

1. **Situation Analysis** — what exactly is the computational claim? Failure may be `UNDERCONSTITUTED`, not impossibility.
2. **Local Possibility Analysis** — what is possible in this constituted situation? Includes exact-scope achievability/impossibility and resource/frontier analysis.
3. **Comparative / Transport Analysis** — what changes, refines, substitutes or transports when situations/capabilities change? Includes reduction/hardness, capability substitution and regime comparison.
4. **Applicability Analysis** — what does a formal result license about an actual owner-scoped target now?

Complexity/frontier, impossibility guards, regime comparison, capability substitution and reduction/hardness remain specializations rather than new peer product ontologies.

`SameUsePolicyAdmission != CapabilitySubstitutability`.

A live currentness-sensitive case observed one scoped egress route/profile unavailable while a separate local Runtime witness succeeded. CP correctly separated:

- narrow constructive existence that can `APPLY` from one implemented actual witness;

- `HistoricalCapability != CurrentCapability`;
