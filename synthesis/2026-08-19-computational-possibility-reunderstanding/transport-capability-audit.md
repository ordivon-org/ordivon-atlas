# CP Destructive Audit — Transport, Capability, Quantifier and Composition Results

## Status

This file compresses Rounds 1–11 of `task:computational-possibility-achievability-quantifier-audit-20260819`. It is an anti-rediscovery index, not a substitute for owner-native `DERIVED-TRANSPORT-CAPABILITY-CALCULUS.md`.

## 1. Quantifier and dependency audit

Central result:

`Existential engine survives only as a guarded strategy normal form.`

A vacuous `exists dummy: opaqueSat` encoding is not acceptable when it erases theorem-bearing dependency structure.

Surviving controls:

- `QuantifierStructure != OpaqueSatisfactionPredicate`;
- `GlobalPackaging != GlobalInformationAccess`;
- `WitnessExistence != WitnessDiscoverability`.

The temporary Quantifier–Dependency Signature was useful as an audit lens but did not earn canonical primitive/schema standing.

## 2. Composition audit

Separate existence does not give composite existence:

`Ach(C1) and Ach(C2) !=> Ach(C1 o C2)`.

Composition may require a cross-coordinate joint-admissibility contract covering semantic interfaces, information visibility, causal dependency, randomness, state and resource aggregation.

Permanent controls:

- `ComponentwiseAchievability != CompositionalAchievability`;
- `StandaloneSatisfaction != ContextualSatisfaction`;
- `TypeCompatibility != DependencyCompatibility`;
- `PerComponentProbability != JointProbability`;
- `NamedWitnessConstruction != ParametricReduction`.

## 3. Presentation / representation audit

Absolute presentation invariance was falsified.

Replacement:

`Qualified Presentation Invariance`.

Semantic correspondence may survive while computational possibility/complexity changes because representation, information topology, causal timing or resource scaling changes.

Strength levels remain distinct:

1. semantic correspondence;
2. qualitative computational equivalence;
3. quantitative/resource equivalence;
4. contextual/compositional equivalence.

Permanent controls:

- `SemanticEquivalence != ComputationalEquivalence`;
- `ComputabilityEquivalence != ComplexityEquivalence`;
- `SameFinalInput != SameComputationalSituation`;
- `AxisPlacement != ComputationalTruth`.

## 4. Transport normalization

A flat transport enum such as `{positive, negative, upper, lower, bidirectional, contextual}` was rejected as conceptually ill-typed.

The more stable base object is a typed witness/capability-preserving transport/evidence arrow between constituted scopes.

From one sound witness-preserving arrow:

- achievability transports forward;
- impossibility transports backward by contraposition.

Quantitative upper claims additionally need forward domination; quantitative lower claims need reverse candidate coverage/reflection.

Therefore:

- `TheoremDirection != EvidenceDirection` where applicable;
- `OneWaySimulation != ComputationalEquivalence`;
- `QuantitativeLower != QualitativeContrapositionOnly`.

## 5. Scope / promise / reduction direction

Promise/domain scope is theorem-bearing.

For many reductions, instance/obligation translation and solver/capability lift run in opposite directions.

Permanent controls:

- `InstanceMapDirection != WitnessLiftDirection`;
- `OverlapCorrectness != PromiseCoverage`;
- `AssumptionStrength != ObligationStrength`;
- `RegimePowerOrder != ContractDemandOrder`;
- `ContractDemandOrder != ComputationalHardnessOrder`.

No universal scalar `stronger/weaker/harder` order survived.

## 6. Promise-oracle pressure

At least three access semantics were distinguished:

- strict/validated promise access;
- loose access with outside-promise behavior unconstrained but client-robust;
- completion-consistent access.

The major derived distinction was:

`PromiseProblemSolver != OracleCapability`.

Turing-style composition may require a higher-order capability lift:

`Cap(C) -> Cap(B) -> Sol(A)`

rather than merely `Sol(C) -> Sol(B)`.

Permanent controls:

- `PromiseCorrectness != OracleSubstitutability`;
- `UndefinedOracleAnswer != ChosenOracleAnswer`;
- `ExistenceOfFavorableCompletion != ValidLooseOracleReduction`.

## 7. Relational / multivalued providers

A valid-output relation does not determine selection authority or interaction semantics.

For a generic provider-parametric capability, correctness must hold for the complete class of admitted provider behaviors/realizers, not one lucky selector.

Permanent controls:

- `ValidOutputRelation != SelectionSemantics`;
- `OneSelectorReduction != ProblemLevelReduction`;
- `RelationalOutput != AdversarialChoice`.

Existential, provider-parametric, strategic and stochastic choice modes must be constituted explicitly.

## 8. Stateful capability pressure

Pointwise-valid outputs do not determine whole-history validity.

Examples included sticky selection, monotone counters, commitment protocols and resource-conservation constraints.

Permanent controls:

- `PointwiseValidity != HistoryValidity`;
- `FinitePrefixValidity != LivenessValidity`.

State-machine, trace, fair-trace and other presentations remain interchangeable only under qualified preservation; hidden implementation state is not automatically CP-semantic.

## 9. Stochastic capability pressure

Equal per-call marginals or support do not determine interactive stochastic capability.

Fresh versus shared randomness produced different transcript-level success despite identical per-call Bernoulli marginals.

Permanent controls:

- `PerCallMarginals != JointLaw`;
- `SupportEquivalence != StochasticCapabilityEquivalence`;
- `StaticOutputDistribution != InteractiveStochasticCapability`.

Adaptive interaction is better understood through a theorem-appropriate causal law/kernel/strategy plus explicit probability/expectation/tail aggregation.

## 10. Capability interaction contract

The derived umbrella `Capability Interaction Contract` survived promise-oracle, relational, stateful and stochastic pressure.

It remains technology-neutral and non-mandatory. Depending on the theorem, load-bearing structure may include:

- input/environment assumptions;
- operation/query scope;
- response obligations;
- role/choice authority;
- information/visibility/causality;
- history/state/consistency;
- operational totality/termination;
- fairness/liveness;
- stochastic law/correlation;
- quantitative error/resource profile.

No universal fixed schema was admitted.

## 11. Refinement and substitution

Raw behavior-set inclusion is insufficient when provider assumptions differ.

Safe substitution has assumption/guarantee polarity:

- replacement must not require stronger client/environment assumptions;
- under admitted assumptions, replacement must not weaken required guarantees.

When assumptions/types align, a narrower admitted provider behavior/law class is a stronger provider guarantee.

Permanent controls:

- `FewerBehaviors != StrongerProvider` without aligned assumptions/scopes;
- `ProviderGuaranteeStrength != ClientRobustnessStrength`.

- Scope/Promise/Oracle/Search/State/Trace/Stochastic/Capability Foundations;

Final architectural standing:

- `NumberedFoundationCount = 0`;
