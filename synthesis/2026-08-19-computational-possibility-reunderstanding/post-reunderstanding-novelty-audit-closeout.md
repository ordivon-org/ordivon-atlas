## 1. What should be retained from the 24-round re-understanding

### 1.1 Stable seven-family inferential skeleton

Most surviving CP anti-laws and positive laws compress into seven recurring families:

1. **Constitution** — claim formation precedes possibility judgment; `Underconstituted != Impossible`.
2. **Admissibility** — possibility quantifies over admissible agency, not arbitrary mathematical existence.
3. **Possibility Polarity / Coverage** — positive existence may need one witness; impossibility requires coverage of the declared candidate space.
4. **Multi-Order Comparison** — assumption strength, obligation demand, provider guarantee, client robustness, regime power, hardness and resource order must not be collapsed into one `stronger` relation.
5. **Typed Theorem Variance** — the theorem action licensed by a native relation depends on direction, scope, preservation strength, quantitative distortion and claim polarity.
6. **Earned Composition** — local achievability/admissibility does not automatically become joint achievability/admissibility.
7. **Formal–Actual Applicability** — formal truth becomes a bounded actual conclusion only through a current, scope-preserving, evidence-backed bridge.

These are not seven Foundations. They are recurring inferential invariants.

### 1.2 Four high-level illegal collapses

A large fraction of CP failure ecology can be compressed into four collapse classes:

- **Scope Collapse** — expanding a result from one promise/model/target/time scope to a broader one without coverage.
- **Type / Projection Collapse** — treating equality under one projection (semantic, API, marginal, benchmark, interface) as equivalence of the richer computational possibility structure.
- **Quantifier / Coverage Collapse** — replacing `exists` with `forall`, local evidence with exhaustive exclusion, or tested cases with full candidate coverage.
- **Formal–Actual Boundary Collapse** — turning a formal theorem into current operational truth without applicability evidence.

This is a stronger anti-rediscovery compression than preserving dozens of isolated `A != B` statements.

### 1.3 CP is ontologically thin and relationally dense

The 24-round reconstruction repeatedly failed to make Capability, Oracle, State, Randomness, Promise, Resource, Trace or similar important nouns deletion-essential primitives. Their theorem-bearing content could be recovered through constituted situations, admissibility, satisfaction, interaction/process structure and typed relations.

The durable interpretation is therefore:

> **CP's deletion-essential content is more law-like and relational than noun-like.**

This strengthens, but does not academically novelize, the zero-Foundation standing.

### 1.4 Inferential unification, not representation unification

CP should not require specialist computational theories to serialize into one universal object language. The more stable ambition is:

> **specialist theories retain local ontology and native relation truth; CP only studies what computational possibility conclusions those native relations license across boundaries.**

This directly rejects universal CP DSL/schema/graph/category ambitions without a fresh necessity proof.

## 2. External novelty audit — what was materially downgraded

The external comparison materially reduced the set of safe CP originality claims.

### 2.1 Strong existing near-neighbors

- **Weihrauch reducibility / represented-space theory** strongly overlaps problem/solver reduction, uniform computational content, hardness/degrees, composition, representation transport and much of the qualitative-to-quantitative variance observed in the representation × solver-reduction case.
- **Interface / contract / refinement theories** strongly overlap assumption/guarantee capability contracts, contextual substitutability, compatibility, refinement, composition and quantitative interface refinement.
- **Abstract Interpretation / correctness–incorrectness traditions** strongly overlap abstraction preservation, over/under approximation, and much of the positive-witness versus negative-coverage polarity.
- **Institution Theory** is a strong architectural near-neighbor for heterogeneous formalisms, typed translations and satisfaction preservation; therefore `heterogeneous formalisms + translation + invariance` is not a safe CP novelty claim.
- **Contract realizability / synthesis / repair** weakens realizability and `find the missing part / repair the specification` as standalone CP novelty claims.
- **Lawvere-doctrine / categorical reducibility work** weakens any claim that generic categorical unification of Turing/Medvedev/Muchnik/Weihrauch-like reducibilities is novel CP territory.

## 3. Two hostile mixed-transport tests and the corrections they forced

### 3.1 Representation translation × solver reduction

The test produced a stable variance ladder:

`semantic preservation`
`-> effective name translation`
`-> qualitative computability preservation`
`-> uniform translation`
`-> quantitative/complexity preservation`
`-> family-level hardness preservation`.

Important anti-collapse:

- `ComputableRepresentationEquivalence != PolytimeRepresentationEquivalence`;
- `OneReductionPreserved != HardnessPreserved`.

But the decisive destructive result is that represented-space / Weihrauch / second-order-complexity theory already naturally owns most of this case. It validates CP's theorem-relative transport lens but does **not** establish CP novelty.

### 3.2 Interface refinement × resource lower bound

This test corrected an earlier overstatement.

If native refinement semantically gives:

`Impl(K2) subseteq Impl(K1)`

inside the **same implementation universe** with the **same resource semantics**, then a universal lower bound over `Impl(K1)` automatically holds over the refined subset `Impl(K2)`. No new `resource reflection` artifact is needed.

Extra cross-family bridge burden appears only when native relation semantics and the target theorem are misaligned, for example:

- candidate universe mismatch;
- resource-measure mismatch;
- context mismatch;
- stochastic/history mismatch;
- actual/current target mismatch.

This yields a stronger current principle:

> **Do not add a preservation burden that the native relation semantics already discharges.**

## 4. Narrowed CP technical hypothesis

The strongest remaining hypothesis is no longer `CP owns heterogeneous transports`.

A better formulation is:

> **CP should not own the arrows; it should study what the arrows are allowed to prove.**

More formally, the research object becomes something like:

`NativeRelationEvidence`
`-> consequences already entailed by the native theory`
`-> residual requirements of the target computational theorem`
`-> permitted cross-theory theorem action`.

Useful explanatory equation:

`TheoremTransportBurden = Requirements(TargetTheorem) - Closure(Consequences(NativeRelationEvidence))`.

This is a research hypothesis, not yet a formal calculus.

Possible name for the technical residue:

- `Cross-Family Computational Theorem Variance`;
- or, even narrower, `Cross-Theory Computational Theorem Lifting`.

Do not rename the CP owner around either phrase yet.

## 5. Strong surviving research residues

### 5.1 Residue A — cross-theory / cross-family theorem lifting

Question:

> Given a relation/refinement/reduction/abstraction certificate owned by one specialist theory, what additional conditions—if any—are necessary and sufficient before a theorem family owned by another computational theory may be transported?

The interesting cases are **mixed-universe** cases where no single native theory already owns both the relation semantics and the target theorem semantics.

This residue remains provisional until it survives further comparison with institution morphisms/comorphisms, logical-relations/refinement metatheory, doctrines/fibrations and adjacent meta-theories.

### 5.2 Residue B — current evidence-bearing formal→actual applicability

Question:

> When may a formal achievability/impossibility/resource theorem be lifted to a specific current actual target whose capabilities, routes, implementations and premises are externally owned and time-sensitive?

The surviving candidate distinction is not merely positive/negative polarity; those have mature formal near-neighbors. The candidate seam is:

`formal theorem`
`+ exact actual target identity`
`+ owner-sourced premise evidence`
`+ currentness`
`+ polarity-appropriate candidate/quantitative coverage`
`-> bounded actual conclusion`.

This is currently the strongest next hostile-test target.
