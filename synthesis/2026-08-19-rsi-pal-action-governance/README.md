## Why this collection exists

The inquiry subsequently moved through action admission, hidden premises, structural uncertainty, consequence propagation, causal gain, gain conversion, conversion-rule change, constitutional replacement, plural grounding, and procedural finality.

The durable result is not one universal theory. The strongest repeated pattern is a family of anti-lifting / anti-collapse constraints that prevent one kind of standing, capability, success, finality, or visibility from silently acquiring another kind of authority or causal reach.

## 4. Decision under structural uncertainty

The conversation distinguished:

- risk: outcome uncertainty within represented states;
- ambiguity / model uncertainty: uncertainty over represented models/parameters;
- structural uncertainty: relevant states, actors, consequences, dependencies, or premises may be absent from the representation.

Therefore:

- `RobustOptimization != RobustToUnknownStructure`.
- A catch-all tail probability does not close an omitted-state problem.
- `MoreSteps != MoreReversibility`; staged action is useful only when it actually delays important commitment / visibility boundaries.
- `ImplementationMagnitude != ConsequenceMagnitude`.
- `Cancel != Rollback`; kill/abort limits future propagation, not already committed external effects.
- `OneUnknown != GlobalFreeze`; uncertainty should block its actual dependency cone, not unrelated work.

The strongest structural response was not one risk score, but a combination:

1. bound initial commitment;
2. bound known propagation channels;
3. preserve maneuverability / options where justified;
4. maintain Reality re-contact / reconciliation;
5. retain explicit `UNKNOWN` rather than fabricate closure.

Candidate method-level statement:

When unknown-unknown risk cannot be reliably priced, control the **irreversible commitment surface** rather than pretending to estimate a complete risk distribution.

## 6. Causal gain and recursive amplification

Propagation is not itself gain.

The conversation separated:

- reach gain — influence reaches more loci;
- magnitude gain — effect size grows;
- generative gain — effects increase or preserve future effect-generating capacity.

Strong anti-laws:

- `Cycle != Source`.
- `PositiveFeedback != UnboundedAmplification`.
- `Persistence != Gain`.
- `InformationReplication != AgencyReplication`.
- `Delegation != AuthorityCopy`.
- `SelfSustaining != SelfCertifying`.
- `RecursiveDepth != ImprovementDepth`.
- `HighLeverage != HighValue`.

Generative gain becomes recursively important when an effect modifies future action-generation, selection, evaluation, observation, authority, resource, memory, or replication machinery.

The dangerous case is often **gain coupling**: capability, authority, resources, evidence standing, persistence, replication, and evaluator control convert into each other in a reinforcing loop.

Damping mechanisms are typed rather than universal: resource exhaustion, delegation attenuation, saturation, negative feedback, independent revalidation/currentness, and contraction all damp different gain channels.

## 7. Gain conversion / causal-capital exchange

A further repair was required: there is no single fungible `causal capital` stock.

Observed types — capability, evidence, standing, authority, resources, reputation/attention, persistence, action permission — have different semantics.

Strong survivor:

`PossessionOfOneKindOfCausalCapacityDoesNotByItselfMintAnotherKind`.

Legal / supported conversion requires a bridge:

`A --Bridge(A->B)--> B`.

Examples:

- Evidence -> Standing requires provenance, applicability, sufficiency, currentness.
- Standing -> Action requires use-specific premises, authority, mandate, feasibility.
- Performance evidence may support a separate broader-admission decision; `Performance != Authority`.
- Capability -> Authority has no automatic bridge; it needs valid delegation/admission/constitutive basis.

Candidate anti-self-lifting condition:

A high-reach conversion bridge should not be created solely under the control of the source capital seeking conversion.

The deepest synthesis here is:

`SemanticCollapseCanCreateSpuriousGainConversionEdges`.

Hence, in this bounded sense, `SemanticDeCollapse` can function as `CausalGainDecoupling`: splitting `Capability != Authority`, `Evidence != Standing`, `Standing != Action`, `Projection != Truth`, etc. removes illicit zero-cost conversion edges.

## Cross-cutting compression

The most stable family across this conversation is not a new monolithic theory, but repeated refusal of **lifting**:

- evidence does not lift itself into standing;
- standing does not lift itself into action permission;
- capability does not lift itself into authority;
- local effect does not lift itself into downstream boundedness;
- success does not lift itself into legitimacy;
- persistence does not lift itself into truth/currentness;
- current settlement does not lift itself into reason/truth finality;
- a new rule does not lift itself into valid authority merely by declaring a new admission basis.

One compact interpretation is **standing-matched commitment**: epistemic, operational, structural, authority, resource, and temporal commitment should not outrun the typed standing actually earned for that commitment. This remains candidate synthesis, not an admitted universal law.
