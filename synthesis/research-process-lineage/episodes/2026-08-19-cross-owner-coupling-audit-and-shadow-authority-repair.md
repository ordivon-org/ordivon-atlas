Semantic decoupling often means **contracting authority and separating truth roles**, not moving files, repositories or services.

The final law is:

`Localize instance truth; generalize only deletion-essential reusable relations; constrain carriers with semantic ceilings; compose cross-owner behavior through justified typed bridges; rehome only after historical-home deletion testing.`
