## Initial pressure

The inquiry started from the observation that a finite intelligence need not wait passively for Reality to produce contradictions. It can actively obtain discriminating pressure through external science, historical anomalies, interventions, new measurements/sensors, query changes, representation changes, countermodels and first-principles constructions.

The explicit guardrail was to keep the problem open and not collapse it into active learning, scientific discovery, Representation Collapse, Epistemic Organization, information theory, control, ML, philosophy or an Ordivon project.

## Load-bearing expansion chain

1. **Interface-relative blindness** — more data inside one interface does not repair missing observation/intervention/model dimensions.
2. **Basis challenge bootstrap** — a failure invisible to the current monitor basis cannot generate a failure-specific endogenous trigger; bounded partly-exogenous challenge prevents permanent sovereignty by one basis.
3. **Cross-basis admission** — local empirical bridges can compare incommensurable bases without a neutral total language; pursuit admission is weaker than truth admission.
4. **Reality-contact allocation** — standard experimental design optimizes inside known support; open support requires protection against incumbent evidence starvation and recognition of capability-building/irreversibility costs.
5. **Evidence uptake** — observation validity, interpretation and theory revision must be separated so anomalous Reality traces can survive before a new ontology exists.
6. **Anomaly escalation** — basis pressure is better read from repair-trajectory morphology than residual magnitude alone.
7. **Repair potential** — plateau/degeneration signals do not certify programme exhaustion; repair operators, capabilities and exposures themselves can be experimentally changed.
8. **Problem-space generativity** — solving and generating questions inside one language does not imply expansion of what can become a problem.
9. **Relevance formation** — no context-free future-importance oracle is available under unrestricted future goals/capabilities; future recoverability is more tractable than future-importance prediction.
10. **Candidate individuation** — relevance selection presupposes formation of candidates; candidate decompositions are lossy inductive hypotheses about sameness/difference.
11. **Identity/invariance** — sameness is scope- and probe-relative; false merge and false split are symmetric, and identity may drift or fail transport.
12. **Transformation formation** — testing invariance under known transformations is weaker than discovering transformations, which is weaker than expanding the transformation language itself.
13. **Comparability formation** — deep correspondence requires candidate juxtaposition; retrieval/gating can erase the opportunity before structural mapping occurs.
14. **Developmental comparability** — successful comparisons can form relational schemas that become new retrieval coordinates, making previously remote analogies reachable through intermediate steps.
15. **Current frontier** — intermediate artifacts may matter because they transform future search geometry even when present truth/utility/explanatory payoff is weak.

## Repeated negative boundary

Across layers, fixed support is not rescued merely by more optimization inside that support. This appears in hypothesis spaces, monitoring bases, experiment generators, question languages, relevance candidates, candidate partitions, transformation grammars and comparison gates. The recurrence is a useful retrieval pattern but is **not** admitted as one universal ontology.

## Important corrections preserved

- Finer distinction is not automatically progress; legitimate merge/unification can remove spurious differences.
- Compression is not automatically representation collapse; collapse is harmful when removed distinctions later carry separating consequences.
- Randomness prevents some permanent zero-support lock-in but does not generate missing semantics.
- Incommensurability is not empirical incomparability.
- No anomaly does not imply no new problem; new tools/capabilities can create problem space without prior failure.
- Invariance is evidence for scoped equivalence, not a universal identity certificate.
- Current similarity is not comparability; remote structural analogies may be developmentally reachable.
