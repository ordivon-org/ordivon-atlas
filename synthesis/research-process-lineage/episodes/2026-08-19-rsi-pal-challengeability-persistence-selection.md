## Starting pressure

The inquiry resumed from a narrow question: if two representations or epistemic bases agree, what has actually been challenged? Surface diversity, multiple Agents, multiple artifacts or multiple representations were not assumed to imply independent evidence.

The first repair was failure-hypothesis-relative:

`RepresentationDiversity != EpistemicIndependence`.

A basis change is valuable only to the extent that it attacks a plausible common-mode failure path. This separated support provenance from challenge provenance and led to a bounded claim/challenge profile rather than a scalar confidence score.

## Challenge-profile pressure

Normative and Harness dogfood showed the same structural point from different sides. A claim can have strong current standing while important challenge dimensions remain untested; many tests can also share one implementation/corpus/evaluator family.

The conversation therefore separated:

- evidence/support volume;
- failure-mode/challenge diversity;
- claim standing;
- challenge standing;
- known live common modes;
- open-world remainder.

The challenge profile was retained as a research view, not admitted as a new global schema or confidence authority.

## Failure-hypothesis admission

The next problem was preventing infinite generation of speculative ways to be wrong. The inquiry distinguished logical conceivability from active research standing. A useful live failure hypothesis needs a concrete target/path, discrimination, and claim/use relevance; high operational consequence may justify mitigation without truth promotion.

This reused existing owner admission discipline rather than creating a universal new framework.

## Portfolio expansion and destructive subtraction

The inquiry then asked how to choose among several high-standing challenges under limited research budget. Initial distinctions included sequencing, screening, upstream elimination, multi-rival discrimination, capability investment, evidence expiry and irreversibility.

Prior-art comparison removed most independent standing: sequential Bayesian experimental design, value of information, active learning, dynamic programming and real options already cover much of adaptive test ordering and information/action value.

Result:

- `Adaptive Research Portfolio Theory` — NOT ADMITTED;
- `TreeShapingValue` as a new primitive — REJECTED;
- within-model optimization — import mature theory rather than reinvent.

## Open-world model adequacy and second subtraction

The surviving pressure moved upward: what if the hypothesis/problem/measurement grammar itself is too narrow? Open-world learning, automated scientific discovery, open-ended problem generation, autoformulation and philosophy of scientific discovery/conceptual change again absorbed a large fraction of the apparent novelty.

The remaining practical methodological pressure was bounded counter-basis challengeability, not epistemic completeness:

`OpenEndedSearch != OpenEndedRepresentability`.

The system should preserve ways to question the generator/basis that defines what problems and counterexamples can be generated, without claiming an ultimate open-world generator.

## PAL / RSI reinterpretation

This produced a direct revision of the PAL/RSI mental model. A persistent adaptive loop can become extremely effective while reinforcing one observation/problem/evaluation basis. Recursive optimization therefore does not automatically establish open-world RSI.

The conversation separated:

- object-level improvement;
- improvement-process improvement;
- basis-challenge/rechallenge capacity.

Challengeability was retained as a necessary safeguard/capacity for open-world RSI, not redefined as RSI itself.

Existing Ordivon separation laws were then re-read as anti-short-circuit boundaries: execution cannot mint domain success; evidence cannot mint standing; projection cannot mint authority; support cannot mint sufficiency; history cannot mint currentness.

## Functional-topology diagnosis

Reprojecting Ordivon by PAL/RSI function rather than repository showed high coverage of semantic roles but weaker coverage of adaptive transitions.

The most important diagnosis was:

`SemanticTopology strong; AdaptiveTransitionTopology uneven`.

Three recurrent weak transitions were identified:

1. transient cognition/research -> durable research delta;
2. research delta -> engineering/consumer disposition;
3. research episode -> future research-policy change.

The conversation explicitly rejected solving this by creating a central `PAL Engine` or meta-owner.

This produced a separate persistence axis:

`EpistemicStanding != PersistenceStanding`.
