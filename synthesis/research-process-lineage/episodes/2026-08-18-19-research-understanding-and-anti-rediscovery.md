## Question

After many owner-specific research programmes had accumulated, what knowledge was still missing even though the detailed owner results and Git/Host provenance already existed?

## Initial position

The durable system already had substantial owner corpora, closeouts, Host continuity, Git history and Atlas currentness projections. A natural assumption was that preserving those final authoritative results plus provenance would be enough; conversation-level explanations could remain transient.

## Pressure that exposed the gap

During a broad research review/explanation conversation, high-value understanding emerged **between** owner results:

- a compact responsibility/worldview map across Reality, Meaning, Possibility, Cognition, Realization, Work, Synthesis, Map, Investigation and Current Truth;
- a human-readable compression of the research method;
- owner-topology explanation showing why truth-condition ownership differs from repository partitioning;
- a Host/Harness/Runtime/domain-result realization guide;
- a research-significance map separating world-model change, method change, formalization and frontier value;
- a Human–Ordivon learning/knowledge-placement map;
- explicit anti-rediscovery rules for checking prior results before opening fresh research;
- an assimilation manifest distinguishing what was already durable, newly synthesized, useful explanation, and deliberately not copied.

Much of the underlying truth was already durable upstream, but the **semantic compression itself** was not. Reconstructing it required repeatedly reading many owners and re-deriving the relationships.

## Rival / rejected simplifications

### Rival A — owner corpora are sufficient

Rejected as a complete answer for discovery/comprehension. Owner corpora remain authoritative, but they intentionally optimize owner-native truth rather than cross-owner mental compression.

### Rival B — store the full conversation

Rejected. Verbatim dialogue contains too much transient prose and does not acquire semantic authority merely by being stored.

### Rival C — make Atlas a new semantic owner

Rejected. Atlas must remain a generated/projection/discovery system; synthesis cannot overwrite owner truth.

### Rival D — copy everything useful into synthesis

Rejected by the later assimilation pass. The conversation was explicitly deduplicated into `ALREADY_DURABLE`, `NEW_CROSS_OWNER_SYNTHESIS`, `HUMAN_CONSUMPTION_GUIDE`, `CONVERSATION_ONLY_EXPLANATION`, and `NOT_COPIED` categories so that useful prose would not become a parallel mutable truth corpus.

## Surviving insight

The durable gap was not “missing domain truth.” It was **missing reusable compression and navigation across already-durable truths**.

The anti-rediscovery result further sharpened this: before opening fresh research, Ordivon should distinguish at least `NEW_QUESTION`, `SEMANTICALLY_EXISTING`, `HISTORICAL_OR_SUPERSEDED`, `REPAIR_OF_EXISTING`, `CROSS_OWNER_SYNTHESIS_NEEDED`, and `CURRENTNESS_UNKNOWN` rather than treating absence from current working memory as novelty.

The assimilation manifest added a second anti-duplication law: not every useful conversation segment deserves a new durable semantic document. Some material should remain pointer-only or deliberately `NOT_COPIED`.

## New tension discovered

Even after the richer assimilation pass, one important class remained underrepresented: **research-process history**.

Publication history or a synthesis manifest can tell us that an authority or understanding changed. It does not necessarily tell us:

- what the old belief was;
- why it was reasonable;
- what hostile case attacked it;
- which evidence changed the standing;
- what survived the repair;
- whether an old instruction became stale because it was wrong, or because the world/currentness changed.

That gap motivates the present `research-process-lineage/` manual layer.

## Current standing

The existing `synthesis/2026-08-18-19-research-understanding/` collection is current as non-authoritative curated synthesis, subject to revalidation if upstream owners materially advance.

The process-lineage extension is an experiment/manual requirement, not yet an Atlas schema or generated view.
