## Starting question

The project had already achieved a clean zero-Foundation architecture, eight-coordinate Computational Situation coverage schema and derived achievability theory.

The new question was more uncomfortable:

> If the research had become so rich, why did the canonical project still risk looking like an eight-field schema plus a Boolean `Achievable` predicate? Had important structure been compressed away, or had the project found the right thin core?

The investigation deliberately avoided assuming that project formation meant conceptual understanding was finished.

## Transition 1 — existential engine under hostile quantifier pressure

### Initial canonical form

`Achievable(C) iff exists w in Adm(C) such that w satisfies O/Phi under C`.

### Hostile cases

The audit tested:

- pointwise versus uniform quantification;
- adversarial alternation;
- randomized probability scope;
- private/public randomness visibility;
- interactive roles;
- family quantification;
- discoverability versus existence.

### Revision

The existential form survived, but only as a **guarded strategy normal form**.

Load-bearing quantifier/dependency structure must remain explicit/recoverable.

Surviving law:

`QuantifierStructure != OpaqueSatisfactionPredicate`.

No new existence primitive or Foundation survived.

## Transition 2 — composition and presentation attacks

Separate component witnesses were shown insufficient for composite achievability without joint/contextual admissibility.

Then absolute presentation invariance was explicitly falsified: semantically equivalent descriptions can have different computational consequences if representation, information topology, causal structure or resource scaling changes.

Revision:

- composition is a cross-coordinate admissibility burden;
- presentation invariance must be qualified by theorem-family-sensitive preservation/transport;
- the eight coordinates are responsibility/coverage projections, not proven orthogonal/minimal dimensions.

## Transition 3 — transport normalization

An early attempt to classify transport using a flat enum such as positive/negative/upper/lower/bidirectional/contextual was rejected.

The stable object became a typed witness/capability-preserving transport between constituted claim scopes.

One such evidence arrow can induce:

- achievability forward;
- impossibility backward;
- quantitative upper transfer with domination;
- quantitative lower transfer with candidate coverage/reflection.

This clarified reduction directionality, scope coverage, composition and theorem variance without introducing a Transport Foundation.

## Transition 4 — promise-oracle, relational, stateful and stochastic capability pressure

The audit progressively widened provider semantics.

### Promise oracle

`PromiseProblemSolver != OracleCapability`.

Turing-style composition may require higher-order capability substitution rather than one-shot solver construction.

### Relational / multivalued

`ValidOutputRelation != SelectionSemantics`.

Generic substitution must be robust to the admitted provider behavior/realizer class, not one favorable selector.

### Stateful

`PointwiseValidity != HistoryValidity` and `FinitePrefixValidity != LivenessValidity`.

Whole-history constraints, state evolution, commitment, conservation and fairness may be theorem-bearing.

### Stochastic

`PerCallMarginals != JointLaw` and `SupportEquivalence != StochasticCapabilityEquivalence`.

Adaptive interaction may require causal stochastic law/kernel semantics rather than static output distributions.

### Surviving synthesis

A technology-neutral derived umbrella — **Capability Interaction Contract** — survived all four pressures.

It remained a derived explanatory/theorem surface, not a mandatory schema, new axis or Foundation.

## Transition 5 — deletion/factorization audit

## Transition 7 — positive reconstruction of the project referent

After destructive pressure, the project was rebuilt positively.

The key conceptual shift was:

`CP != theory of Achievable predicate`.

`C -> Poss(C)`.

- `Achievable` = non-emptiness projection.
