## Question

What is the project historically called Network actually studying after the Communicability carrier repair, and what practical engineering consequences follow if its true referent is broader than communication/forwarding?

## Initial position

The project had a mature Network-labelled theory stack: NDF0–NDF5, Core Theory v1 and multiple derived theories. The name still psychologically suggested nodes, links, messages, routes and forwarding, even though the repaired carrier had already become `InterLocusCapability`.

A rename to **Ordivon Interlocus** was proposed because the semantic scope had outrun the old label.

## Trigger / pressure

The decisive historical pressure was not naming aesthetics. `Communicability` had already failed as a universal carrier under relational/n-ary capability cases; `InterLocusCapability` survived. This raised two questions:

1. what exactly is a `locus` if it is not simply a network node?
2. does Interlocus retain an independent deletion-essential semantic core after subtracting World, Runtime, Harness, SCD, Normative and conventional networking?

## Investigation 1 — locus reconstruction

Hostile cases tested node/entity/spatial/global-partition definitions, multi-locus single-substrate, multi-substrate single-locus, migration, n-ary relational capability, nested/overlapping cuts and temporal/prospective roles.

Revision:

- non-collapsibility was demoted from positive definition to adequacy/essentiality test;
- current positive capability was removed as a locus prerequisite;
- locus became a thin scope/query/cut-qualified projected operational role;
- `Role != Binding != Standing`;
- no global canonical locus set/partition;
- **No Locus Foundation**.

## Investigation 2 — independent-owner deletion tests

Attempts to absorb Interlocus into World, Runtime, Harness, SCD, Normative, conventional networking and distributed-systems framing each lost a distinct proof role.

Surviving residue:

> lawful formation, transformation and warrant of operational capability under qualified separation.

This supported independent owner identity while keeping neighboring predicates externally owned.

## Investigation 3 — explanatory core-law extraction

A six-law compression was initially proposed.

### Falsifier A — witness grounding

A system could satisfy typed witness presence while relying on a self-asserted/stale/out-of-scope witness. C3 was too weak.

Revision: **Admissibly Grounded Witnessed Formation**.

### Falsifier B — evidence conflict

A system could satisfy C1–C6 yet use last-write-wins over admissible conflicting evidence and manufacture positive standing.

Revision: add **C7 Conservative Warrant**.

### Further repair — recursion

Global acyclicity was too strong because grounded recursive/fixed-point semantics are legitimate.

Revision: C6 became **Well-Founded Warrant**.

Final explanatory basis: C1 Qualified Separation; C2 Typed Capability Non-Reduction; C3 Admissibly Grounded Witnessed Formation; C4 Semantic Transport Requires Witness; C5 Continuation-Closed Abstraction; C6 Well-Founded Warrant; C7 Conservative Warrant.

Standing: explanatory interpretation only, not Core Theory v2.

## Investigation 4 — engineering consumption

Runtime, Workstation/Finance, Harness and Research System were audited.

Unexpected negative result: most existing systems already preserved the important distinctions. Interlocus did **not** justify a broad theory-shaped refactor.

Positive residue: a common diagnostic grammar could explain where a target-relative capability warrant first became missing/stale/conflicted without centralizing owner truth.

## Investigation 5 — Capability Path Explanation

Finance egress and Research READY/current-recovery fixtures forced a source-fenced warrant DAG rather than a linear network path.

Harness provided the third-domain choice test:

`BranchFailure != TargetFailure`.

A route can be rejected under one use contract yet admitted under another; blockers require target/necessity semantics before upward propagation.

Result: **Capability Path Explanation v0**, read-only and source-authoritative by reference.

## Investigation 6 — Counterfactual Repair

The next question was not `what failed?` but `what intervention would restore the same target?`

The research separated:

`FailedPremise != TargetBlocker != RequiredConditionChange != NecessaryIntervention != SufficientIntervention != MinimalRepair != PreferredAction`.

Additional repairs:

- `TargetRevision != TargetRepair`;
- `ActualFact != CounterfactualEffect != ActualPostExecutionFact`;
- multi-intervention composition needs joint effect/order witnesses;
- target sufficiency does not imply safety, permission or preference;
- stale counterfactual proof does not imply current execution admission;
- actual restoration requires post-action observation/revalidation.

Result: **Counterfactual Repair Explanation v0**, model-relative and non-executing.

## Investigation 7 — consumer-value dogfood

A bounded 14-claim rubric and reference validator narrowed the practical claim.

The value was not primarily fewer owner reads. Simple native facts often needed no Interlocus projection.

The durable value was:

> machine-enforceable control over cross-proof-role semantic joining.

Reference rule:

`NoWitness -> NoCrossRoleConclusion`.

A caller-supplied validator/projector reached 19/19 focused hostile/positive tests, but production/API standing remained unadmitted.

## Investigation 8 — action-admission deletion

A third generic ActionAdmissionExplanation was considered.

Deletion test showed present-tense `ExecutePlanNow(P)` is simply another Capability Path target use. Technical repair, protected constraints, Normative disposition, selection, execution authority/currentness, occurrence and restored standing remain distinct source roles.

The irreducible residual was **selection among multiple sufficient/admissible repairs**, which belongs to Harness/consumer selector semantics plus external objective/risk/preference and Normative constraints.

Final non-collapse:

`SufficientRepair != Permitted != Preferred != Selected != CurrentlyExecutable != Executed != RestoredStanding`.

`Required != Capable`.

No third Interlocus contract, ActionLease, selector or autonomous repair authority was admitted.

## Current standing

The programme closed with four layers kept separate:

1. frozen owner semantic authority;
2. explanatory locus/Core-Law reinterpretation;
3. read-only engineering explanation contracts;
4. bounded reference validator materialization.

Current policy: **evidence-triggered reopen only**. More examples or implementation enthusiasm are not sufficient.

## Surviving insights

- naming can lag semantic carrier repair;
- owner identity continuity is not display-name continuity;
- abstraction/transport/currentness errors recur across networking, research recovery and Agent action even when domain vocabularies differ;
- cross-owner interoperability can standardize warrant structure without standardizing native truth vocabularies;
- diagnosis, counterfactual sufficiency, normative admission, selection, execution and post-action standing must remain distinct for Agent safety;
- deletion results (`no Foundation`, `no C8`, `no third action contract`, `no production service`) are first-class anti-rediscovery knowledge.
