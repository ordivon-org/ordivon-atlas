## Why these episodes belong together

They demonstrate three different kinds of research value that disappear if Atlas stores only the final current owner map:

### 1. Discovery by decomposition

The current fact `SCD != Computational Possibility` is not merely a classification choice. It resulted from testing a broader Computing umbrella and finding two truth families with independent falsifiers, consumers, change coupling and closure cadence.

Without the process record, a future Agent may see two owners but not know **why one broad owner was rejected** or which alternative splits were also tested and rejected.

### 2. Discovery by supersession without historical erasure

`AlgF0 was genuinely admitted` and `AlgF0 is currently superseded as a numbered Foundation` are simultaneously true in different truth roles.

Without process lineage, a current-only system tends either to erase the historical admission or to preserve the old Foundation as if it were still current.

### 3. Discovery by operational/currentness repair

The earlier plan to publish CP from a non-main research ref was correct while Computing main was dirty/stale. After formation integration and dirty-state resolution, that same instruction became stale. The underlying law survived — `Repository != SemanticOwner` — while the contingent operational workaround did not.

This is neither ordinary falsification nor simple version replacement. It is a **historically valid -> currently stale** transition.
