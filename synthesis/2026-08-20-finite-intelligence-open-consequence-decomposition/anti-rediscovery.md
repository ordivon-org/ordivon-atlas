## Do not reopen as if unresolved

- Unrestricted future tasks eliminate nontrivial universally safe lossy compression.
- Future relevance cannot safely be generated only from current relevance/representation without self-sealing risk.
- Problem space can be revealed, activated and generated; capability growth can create new future epistemic burden.
- Component standing does not compose without a composition warrant and sufficient interface/context assumptions.
- Finite integration tests cannot establish open-world interface completeness.
- MissingInterface != WrongDecomposition != IntrinsicNondecomposability.
- Multiple locally useful decompositions do not generally reduce to one cheap master partition.
- Diagnostic decompositions can act as breakers without becoming operational/ontological commitments.
- Pairwise boundary exposure over declared loci has a logarithmic coding construction, but this does not solve hidden-factor, higher-order or locus-vocabulary blindness.
- Candidate loci are not necessarily objects; reifying a relation/process into a node does not establish objecthood or autonomous intervention semantics.
- Partition search over a fixed locus vocabulary is not open decomposition/locus discovery.
