## 2. Challengeability refinement that survives deduplication

### 2.1 Representation diversity is not epistemic independence

A basis comparison gains standing against a common-mode explanation only when the changed basis actually attacks that failure path.

```text
RepresentationDiversity != EpistemicIndependence
SurfaceOrthogonality != FailureOrthogonality
AgentMultiplicity != FailurePathIndependence
```

The useful question is failure-hypothesis-relative:

> for a candidate common-mode explanation, did the destructive variation actually sever or alter the path by which the observed conclusion could have been manufactured?

This is a refinement of existing falsification/triangulation practice, not a new grand theory.

### 2.2 Support provenance and challenge provenance are different

A conclusion may have many support artifacts while all challenge routes share one corpus, representation family, evaluator, canonicalizer, implementation lineage, or problem generator.

```text
EvidenceVolume != ChallengeDiversity
SupportProvenance != ChallengeProvenance
ClaimStanding != ChallengeStanding
```

The useful projection is therefore not a confidence percentage. For an important claim it can be useful to recover, boundedly:

- which failure/common-mode hypotheses were actually challenged;
- what was varied;
- what remained held fixed;
- what survived/failed;
- which known common modes remain live.

`100% challenge coverage` is not meaningful for open research unless the challenge universe is explicitly finite and closed.

### 2.3 Failure-hypothesis admission is use-relative

A conceivable way to be wrong does not automatically deserve research budget or reopen standing.

```text
ConceivableFailure != ResearchStanding
ResearchStanding != ClaimDefeat
UntestedChallenge != ReopenTrigger
```

A live challenge needs a concrete target/failure path, a discriminating consequence, and relevance to the current claim/use. High consequence may justify mitigation without promoting the hypothesis to truth.

## 4. PAL / RSI reinterpretation

PAL can recursively improve performance while becoming more deeply locked into the basis that defines observation, problem formulation, evaluation and learning. Persistence is therefore both an enabling mechanism and a possible self-reinforcement mechanism.

```text
PersistentAdaptation != EpistemicallyHealthyAdaptation
PerformanceImprovement != ImprovementOfChallengeability
LocalPerformanceRegression != RSIRegression
```

A strong open-world RSI should not be defined as challengeability alone. The surviving requirement is weaker:

> improvement capability should grow without systematically destroying the future capacity to falsify the basis under which improvement is currently measured.

This reinterprets several existing Ordivon separation laws (`Execution != DomainOutcome`, `Evidence != Standing`, `Projection != Authority`, `Historical != Current`, `Support != Sufficiency`, `Possibility != Realization`) as barriers against short self-confirmation loops. This is cross-owner synthesis, not a claim that those owners were originally designed as one PAL theory.

## 8. Persistence-selection bias is a real PAL failure surface

A persistence selector can shape the future question prior. The dangerous false negatives are not random omissions; historical replay shows concentrated risk around material that is structurally inconvenient to summarize:

- unresolved/open pressure;
- negative or not-admitted theory with surviving content;
- owner-ambiguous problems;
- new relations among already-durable components;
- process/revision rationale;
- research-control pivots such as deliberate non-collapse/stop decisions;
- low-compressibility anomalies that have not yet become a clean proposition.

Therefore:

```text
HighCompressibility != HighFutureControlValue
ImmediateActionability != LongTermControlValue
OwnerAmbiguity != LowResearchValue
ComponentsAlreadyKnown != SynthesisAlreadyKnown
NOVELTY_CLOSED != REFERENT_CLOSED
```

The preferred control shape is two-stage:

```text
high-recall temporary candidate capture
    !=
high-precision durable promotion
```

Candidate capture may tolerate recoverable false positives. Durable promotion remains destination/authority-specific and must avoid shadow truth. Classification uncertainty is not automatic discard permission.

A further guard follows from selection bias: evaluate not only stored items but, when recoverable and worthwhile, sample or rechallenge discarded candidates. A selector cannot validate itself solely from the population it chose to retain.
