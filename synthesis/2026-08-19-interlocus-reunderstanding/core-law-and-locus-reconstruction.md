# Interlocus Locus Reconstruction and Core-Law Interpretation v0

## Authority boundary

This is explanatory compression over frozen owner theory. It is **not** Core Theory v2 and does not reopen NDF0–NDF5.

## 1. Why `locus` did not become a Foundation

Hostile reconstruction rejected all of the following as universal definitions:

- locus = node;
- locus = entity;
- locus = spatial point;
- locus = member of one global partition;
- locus iff deleting the distinction changes every capability claim;
- locus requires a current positive capability.

The stable thin interpretation is:

> a locus is a scope/query/cut-qualified projected operational role retained as an available participant coordinate for Interlocus reasoning.

Important separations:

`Role != Binding != Standing`.

`LocusRoleContinuity != BindingContinuity != CapabilityContinuity != StandingContinuity`.

A candidate/prospective role may exist before concrete binding; current usability or serviceability is a later standing question. Overlapping and nested cuts are legitimate. Different projections may preserve different locus distinctions without implying one globally canonical decomposition of Reality.

Result:

`No Locus Foundation`.

Non-collapsibility remains an **adequacy/essentiality test**, not the positive definition of locus.

## 2. Independent-owner deletion tests

Interlocus survived attempted absorption into adjacent owners.

- World absorption fails: a real relation/resource does not by itself establish an operational capability contract.
- Runtime absorption fails: execution occurrence does not establish reusable capability or semantic service satisfaction.
- Harness absorption fails: Agent selection/mediation consumes capability but does not define whether it actually holds.
- SCD absorption fails: description/interface meaning does not establish current operational capability.
- Normative absorption fails: `Can != May != Should`.
- conventional networking is narrower: communication/forwarding/reachability are important specializations, not the universal carrier.
- distributed systems becomes a reduction only by widening so far that it effectively renames the same cross-locus capability problem.

Deletion residue:

> Given qualified separated roles, what capability holds, how may it compose/transform, and what current warrant supports its use?

This supports the independent owner referent **Interlocus Capability**.

## 3. The six-law basis was deliberately falsified

An early compression proposed six laws. Completeness-hostile testing exposed two distinct problems.

### Grounding attack

A typed witness could still be self-asserted, out-of-scope, stale or otherwise inadmissible. Merely requiring `has witness` was too weak.

Repair:

C3 became **Admissibly Grounded Witnessed Formation**.

### Evidence-conflict attack

Within one stable claim epoch, admissible supporting and counterevidence could coexist. C1–C6 did not prevent a system from using last-write-wins to manufacture positive standing.

This produced a genuinely missing law:

C7 **Conservative Warrant**.

The research therefore preserves this causal transition:

`SixLawBasis -> HostileCounterexample -> C3Repair + C7Admission`.

Do not recover C1–C7 as if all seven were obvious from the start.

## 4. C6 was also repaired

Mechanical `Acyclic Warrant` was too strong because valid recursive/fixed-point semantics may contain cycles.

The correct requirement is **Well-Founded Warrant**:

- ungrounded self-supporting proof cycles are invalid;
- separately grounded recursive/fixed-point semantics are allowed;
- time-indexed feedback may enter a later causal/epoch slice;
- future occurrence cannot retroactively self-certify the standing that authorized its own same-slice proof.

Thus:

`RecursiveCycle != InvalidProof`.

`UngroundedSelfSupport = InvalidWarrant`.

## 5. Final explanatory basis

Current Core-Law Interpretation v0:

1. **C1 Qualified Separation** — claims are relative to explicit scope/query/cut-qualified projected locus roles; no universal node/locus ontology is assumed.
2. **C2 Typed Capability Non-Reduction** — InterlocusCapability cannot be collapsed into actuality, meaning, permission, selection, occurrence, evidence/standing, communication, pairwise relation, reachability, forwarding, serviceability or another specialization without an explicit in-scope reduction witness.
3. **C3 Admissibly Grounded Witnessed Formation** — formation/composition/refinement requires admissible source/provenance-grounded witnesses with compatible scope, quantifiers, versions and preserved dependencies/obligations.
4. **C4 Semantic Transport Requires Witness** — migration, rebinding, generation/version/path/projection/epoch/recovery change does not transport standing or obligations for free.
5. **C5 Continuation-Closed Abstraction** — hiding/quotienting/normalization must preserve relevant observation plus admissible future continuation/dependency obligations.
6. **C6 Well-Founded Warrant** — conclusions cannot be supported solely by ungrounded self-dependent proof structure; grounded recursion remains admissible.
7. **C7 Conservative Warrant** — current claim standing is derived from current claim-local authority/completeness policy over an admissible evidence frontier; conflict/unknown is preserved, open-world absence is not negation without closure, record order is not truth, and currentness is not positive standing.

High-compression characterization:

`Formation = C1 + C2 + C3`.

`Transformation/Preservation = C4 + C5`.

`Warrant = C6 + C7`.
