# Foundational Census Round 2B — Identity / Topology Normalization

> Non-authoritative. All canonical root admissions remain **NOT_ADMITTED**.

## Physics

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, OBJECT_REGIME_DOMAIN
- Placements: physical-universe
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: APS maintains many physics subfields with cross-links to astronomy, biology, chemistry, materials and computation. Physics remains a major foundational-domain candidate but not a universal parent for every physically grounded field.

## Chemistry

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, COMPOSITION_TRANSFORMATION_DOMAIN
- Placements: physical-material, molecular
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: IUPAC organizes chemistry into multiple divisions including physical/biophysical, inorganic, organic/biomolecular, polymer, analytical, environment and health-related chemistry, supporting chemistry as a first-class domain with internal methodological and bridge subspaces.

## Condensed matter physics

- Roles: MAJOR_SUBSPACE, MATERIALS_BRIDGE
- Placements: physics, materials
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: APS treats condensed matter as a major physics division while materials physics is separately represented; condensed matter therefore remains a major physics subspace with strong materials overlap.

## Chemical physics

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, MAJOR_SUBSPACE
- Placements: physics, chemistry
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: APS explicitly maintains Chemical Physics and IUPAC maintains Physical and Biophysical Chemistry, demonstrating a stable physics↔chemistry bridge rather than exclusive placement.

## Materials science and engineering

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, MAJOR_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, INTERVENTION_DOMAIN
- Placements: physics, chemistry, biology, engineering
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF and MRS both characterize materials research as inherently interdisciplinary across physics, chemistry, biology and engineering, linking fundamental structure/property understanding to design, synthesis, processing and technological creation.

## Analytical chemistry

- Roles: MAJOR_SUBSPACE, METHODOLOGICAL_AXIS
- Placements: chemistry, measurement
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: IUPAC recognizes Analytical Chemistry as a distinct division. Its focus on measurement/characterization supports a chemistry subspace with methodological-axis character rather than a separate foundational root.

## Polymer science

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, MAJOR_SUBSPACE
- Placements: chemistry, physics, materials, biology-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: Polymer is independently organized in both IUPAC and APS, indicating a stable cross-domain identity spanning chemistry, physics and materials.

## Biology

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, MULTISCALE_LIFE_DOMAIN
- Placements: life
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF BIO organizes fundamental biology from molecular/cellular through organismal and ecological/evolutionary scales, supporting biology as a first-class life-domain candidate with multiple orthogonal scales and processes.

## Molecular and cellular biosciences

- Roles: MAJOR_SUBSPACE, LOWER_SCALE_LIFE_DOMAIN
- Placements: biology, chemistry-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF MCB targets fundamental properties of living systems at atomic, molecular, subcellular and cellular scales; this is a major lower-scale biological subspace, not equivalent to biology as a whole.

## Organismal biology

- Roles: MAJOR_SUBSPACE, SYSTEM_DOMAIN
- Placements: biology, organism
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF IOS explicitly studies organisms as integrated units of biological organization, supporting organismal biology as a major system-level subspace.

## Evolutionary biology

- Roles: PROCESS_AXIS, MAJOR_SUBSPACE
- Placements: biology, ecology, genetics-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF Environmental Biology explicitly supports fundamental evolutionary research across biological entities and scales. Evolution is better modeled as a pervasive biological process axis plus major subspace than as one taxonomic branch among peers.

## Ecology

- Roles: SYSTEM_DOMAIN, MAJOR_SUBSPACE, EARTH_LIFE_BRIDGE
- Placements: biology, environment, earth-life
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF DEB covers species, populations, communities and ecosystems across scales, supporting ecology as a system-oriented life/environment bridge.

## Biological research infrastructure

- Roles: EPISTEMIC_INFRASTRUCTURE
- Placements: biology, methodology
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF DBI funds technologies, facilities and human/research infrastructure for fundamental biology. This should be represented as research infrastructure, not mistaken for a biological object-domain.

## Engineering

- Roles: INTERVENTION_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, DESIGN_REALIZATION_DOMAIN
- Placements: design, technology, physical-realization
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF organizes engineering around chemical/bio/energy/transport, civil/mechanical/manufacturing, and electrical/communications/computing areas, while ABET distinguishes engineering from basic sciences. Engineering is therefore best represented as a design/intervention/realization domain rather than another natural-science object domain.

## Design

- Roles: METHODOLOGICAL_AXIS, CREATIVE_INTERVENTION_DOMAIN
- Placements: engineering, artifacts, systems, human-created-environments
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: The Design Society treats design as creation of artifacts and systems across physical, virtual, psychological, economic and social environments. Design is a cross-domain intervention axis, not reducible to engineering alone.

## Systems engineering

- Roles: INTEGRATIVE_METHOD_AXIS, INTERVENTION_BRIDGE
- Placements: engineering, systems, lifecycle, management-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: INCOSE defines systems engineering as transdisciplinary and integrative across realization, use and retirement of engineered systems; it is a lifecycle integration method/domain rather than a single artifact-class branch.

## Manufacturing

- Roles: REALIZATION_DOMAIN, APPLIED_ENGINEERING_SUBSPACE
- Placements: engineering, materials, production
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF engineering treats manufacturing together with civil/mechanical innovation and material/building technologies, supporting manufacturing as a realization/application domain rather than a foundational science root.

## Bioengineering

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, APPLIED_INTERVENTION_DOMAIN
- Placements: engineering, biology, medicine
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NSF places bioengineering with engineering healthcare while NIH maintains biomedical imaging/bioengineering institutes; bioengineering is a strong biology↔medicine↔engineering bridge.

## Philosophy

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_CONCEPTUAL_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, METHODOLOGICAL_REFLECTIVE_AXIS
- Placements: conceptual, normative, epistemic, historical
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: APA identifies metaphysics, epistemology, logic and ethics as central philosophical areas while emphasizing history, argument construction and expanding cross-disciplinary inquiry. Philosophy is not merely a historical-symbolic bucket.

## History

- Roles: HISTORICAL_INTERPRETIVE_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, TEMPORAL_EVIDENCE_DOMAIN
- Placements: human-past, interpretation, evidence
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: AHA explicitly represents history as a distinct discipline centered on historical thinking and disciplined investigation/interpretation of the past, supporting first-class identity independent of generic humanities.

## Religious studies

- Roles: HISTORICAL_SYMBOLIC_DOMAIN, COMPARATIVE_HUMAN_DOMAIN
- Placements: religion, history, culture, social-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: AAR frames its mission around academic study of religion across world traditions and methodologies. This supports a first-class comparative domain identity but not foundational-root admission in the current pre-social wave.

## Aesthetics

- Roles: CONCEPTUAL_ART_BRIDGE, HUMAN_EXPERIENCE_DOMAIN
- Placements: philosophy, arts, psychology, sociology-boundary, cultural-history
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: ASA explicitly includes philosophical, scientific and theoretical studies of the arts and related experience, including psychology, sociology, anthropology and cultural history. Aesthetics is therefore a bridge identity rather than a philosophy-only child.

## Classics / Classical studies

- Roles: COMPOSITE_HISTORICAL_SYMBOLIC_DOMAIN
- Placements: history, language, literature, philosophy, religion, art, material-culture
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: SCS defines Classics across languages, literatures, history, material culture, philosophy, law, religion and art of the ancient Mediterranean. It is a composite domain, not evidence for a universal foundational root.

## Basic biomedical sciences

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_BRIDGE, TRANSLATIONAL_SOURCE_DOMAIN
- Placements: biology, medicine, human-physiology
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NIH NIGMS explicitly supports basic research on biological processes that underpins diagnosis, treatment and prevention, demonstrating a foundational biology↔medicine bridge distinct from clinical practice.

## Medicine and health sciences

- Roles: APPLIED_PROFESSIONAL_DOMAIN, TRANSLATIONAL_DOMAIN
- Placements: biomedicine, clinical, public-health-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NIH organization is largely disease/body-system/health-mission oriented and spans basic, translational and clinical research. Medicine/health should not be imported wholesale as a foundational object-domain even though it contains foundational biomedical cores.

## Agricultural sciences and systems

- Roles: APPLIED_INTERVENTION_SYSTEM_DOMAIN, EARTH_LIFE_ENGINEERING_BRIDGE
- Placements: biology, ecology, soil, engineering, food, economics-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NIFA agricultural topics combine plants, animals, ecosystems, natural resources, technology, food systems and human/economic concerns. Agriculture is an intervention/system domain drawing on multiple foundations rather than a single foundational root.

## Soil science

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, MAJOR_ENVIRONMENTAL_SUBSPACE
- Placements: earth-science, biology, chemistry, agriculture, climate
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NIFA describes soil as a dynamic natural resource central to plant growth, air/water quality, ecosystems, climate, nutrient cycling and agriculture. Soil science is a strong Earth↔life↔chemistry↔agriculture bridge.

## Positive findings

- **FCR2B-1 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Physics and Chemistry remain strong foundational-domain candidates, but their shared bridge zones such as chemical physics, condensed matter and materials require separate identities rather than exclusive parentage.
- **FCR2B-2 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Materials science and engineering is a major interdisciplinary bridge domain coupling fundamental structure/property inquiry with design, synthesis, processing and technological intervention.
- **FCR2B-3 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Biology is intrinsically multi-axis: molecular/cellular scale, organismal integration, evolutionary processes and ecological systems cannot be represented as one simple branch hierarchy.
- **FCR2B-4 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Engineering is best modeled as an intervention/design/realization domain; design and systems engineering are cross-cutting methodological/integrative axes rather than ordinary natural-science subfields.
- **FCR2B-5 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Philosophy and History deserve distinct first-class identities with different epistemic roles; Historical/Symbolic is therefore a family view, not one domain.
- **FCR2B-6 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Basic biomedical science is a foundational bridge into medicine, while medicine/health as a whole is translational/professional and should not be admitted wholesale as a foundational root.
- **FCR2B-7 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Agriculture is an applied intervention/system domain drawing on life, Earth, chemistry, engineering and social/economic inputs; soil science is a major cross-boundary subspace rather than merely an agricultural topic.

- **FCR2B-N1 [ESTABLISHED_IN_SCOPE]** — NSF Emerging Frontiers is an organizational/funding mechanism, not automatically a knowledge-domain identity.
- **FCR2B-N2 [ESTABLISHED_IN_SCOPE]** — NIH institute boundaries organized around diseases/body systems/missions must not be copied as a foundational biomedical ontology.
- **FCR2B-N3 [ESTABLISHED_IN_SCOPE]** — Engineering accreditation categories and NSF administrative sections do not mint canonical epistemic roots.
