# Foundational Census Round 2C — Residual Normalization

> Seven unresolved Round-1 candidates were revisited. Combined census labels may split; all root admissions remain **NOT_ADMITTED**.

## Mathematics

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_FORMAL_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, ABSTRACT_STRUCTURE_DOMAIN
- Placements: formal-abstract, logic, probability, statistics, computation
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: MSC2020 is a broad researcher-maintained taxonomy of the mathematical sciences. Mathematics warrants first-class formal-domain identity, while its many sections and cross-references argue against reducing all formal sciences to a single simple hierarchy.

## Probability theory and stochastic processes

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_FORMAL_SUBDOMAIN, STOCHASTIC_PROCESS_DOMAIN, BRIDGE_DOMAIN
- Placements: mathematics, statistics, physics, biology, engineering
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: MSC2020 60-XX is a distinct major class with cross-references across mathematics, physics, biology, engineering, systems and information. It is related to but not identical with Statistics.

## Statistics

- Roles: INFERENTIAL_METHOD_AXIS, MAJOR_FORMAL_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, BRIDGE_DOMAIN
- Placements: mathematics, probability, information, biology, medicine, psychology, economics-boundary, engineering
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: MSC2020 62-XX is a distinct major class with foundational topics, decision theory, inference, experiment design and applications across sciences. Statistics is therefore both a formal domain and a cross-domain inferential axis, not synonymous with Probability.

## Dynamical systems and ergodic theory

- Roles: PROCESS_STRUCTURE_AXIS, MAJOR_FORMAL_SUBSPACE, BRIDGE_DOMAIN
- Placements: mathematics, physics, biology, fluid-ocean-atmosphere, control
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: MSC2020 37-XX is distinct and explicitly maps applications to mechanics, fluids, oceanography, meteorology, physics, biology and control. Dynamical systems is a cross-domain process/structure axis, not the same identity as systems theory or systems engineering.

## Systems theory and control

- Roles: FORMAL_SYSTEM_AXIS, BRIDGE_DOMAIN, CONTROL_METHOD_DOMAIN
- Placements: mathematics, control, engineering, computation, stochastic-systems
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: MSC2020 93-XX explicitly identifies systems theory and control, including system structure, stability and stochastic systems. This mathematical/control identity is distinct from lifecycle-oriented Systems Engineering.

## Geochemistry

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, EARTH_SYSTEM_SUBSPACE
- Placements: earth-science, chemistry, atmosphere, hydrosphere, biosphere, planetary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: The Geochemical Society frames geochemistry as applying chemistry to understanding Earth and the solar system, spanning solid Earth, atmospheres, hydrospheres, biospheres and extraterrestrial bodies. It is a stable Earth↔chemistry bridge.

## Physical geography

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, MAJOR_GEOGRAPHIC_SUBDOMAIN, EARTH_HUMAN_INTERFACE_DOMAIN
- Placements: geography, earth-science, environment, geomorphology, climate, biogeography, hydrology, soil, human-environment-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: AAG describes physical geography through geomorphology, climatology, biogeography, hydrology and soil science while emphasizing geography’s porous human/physical boundaries. Physical geography is neither generic Earth science nor merely human geography.

## Genetics

- Roles: INHERITANCE_PROCESS_DOMAIN, BIOLOGICAL_INFORMATION_AXIS, MAJOR_LIFE_SUBSPACE
- Placements: biology, evolution, molecular-cellular, organismal
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NHGRI distinguishes genetics as the study of genes and their roles in inheritance. Genetics therefore has a stable inheritance/process identity within biology and should not be collapsed into Genomics.

## Genomics

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, DATA_INTENSIVE_LIFE_DOMAIN, MAJOR_LIFE_SUBSPACE
- Placements: biology, genetics, computation, data-science, medicine
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: NHGRI distinguishes genomics as study of whole genomes and supports foundational genomic resources, technologies, experiments, data science and analytical tools. Genomics is a genetics-related but distinct biology↔computation↔medicine bridge.

## Information science

- Roles: MAJOR_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, KNOWLEDGE_ORGANIZATION_BRIDGE, APPLIED_INFORMATION_DOMAIN
- Placements: information, computation, cognition, linguistics, classification, communication, knowledge-organization
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: ASIS&T defines information science around properties/flows and the origination, organization, storage, retrieval, interpretation, transmission and use of recorded information, explicitly drawing on computing, cognition, mathematics, logic, linguistics, systems and library science. It warrants first-class identity.

## Librarianship and library studies

- Roles: APPLIED_PROFESSIONAL_DOMAIN, EPISTEMIC_INFRASTRUCTURE, INFORMATION_ACCESS_DOMAIN
- Placements: libraries, information-science, knowledge-organization, archives-boundary, public-access
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: ALA treats librarianship as a professional field prepared through library and information studies programs, with competencies specific to library/information environments. It overlaps information science but is not identical to the scientific inquiry domain.

## Split repairs

- **FCR2C-R1** — `Statistics and probability` → norm:probability, norm:statistics: MSC2020 has separate major classes 60-XX and 62-XX; relation is overlap/cross-reference, not identity.
- **FCR2C-R2** — `Systems and dynamical theory` → norm:dynamical-systems, norm:systems-theory-control: MSC2020 separates 37-XX Dynamical systems and 93-XX Systems theory; control. Systems Engineering remains a third distinct identity.
- **FCR2C-R3** — `Genetics and genomics` → norm:genetics, norm:genomics: NHGRI distinguishes genetics (genes/inheritance) from genomics (whole genomes and genome-scale inquiry).
- **FCR2C-R4** — `Library and information science` → norm:information-science, norm:librarianship: ASIS&T supports a scientific/inquiry identity for Information Science while ALA supports a professional Library and Information Studies/Librarianship identity; overlap does not imply equivalence.

## Findings

- **FCR2C-1 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Mathematics is a first-class formal-domain candidate, but its internal topology already contains domains, bridges and axes; root admission still requires explicit criteria.
- **FCR2C-2 [ESTABLISHED_IN_SCOPE]** — Probability and Statistics are distinct identities; Statistics additionally functions as a cross-domain inferential/methodological axis.
- **FCR2C-3 [ESTABLISHED_IN_SCOPE]** — Dynamical Systems, Systems Theory/Control and Systems Engineering are three distinct identities with specific bridge relations.
- **FCR2C-4 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Geochemistry is a stable Earth↔chemistry bridge spanning planetary, atmospheric, hydrologic and biospheric contexts.
- **FCR2C-5 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Physical Geography is a stable geography↔Earth/environment bridge and should not be collapsed into either generic Earth Science or human geography.
- **FCR2C-6 [ESTABLISHED_IN_SCOPE]** — Genetics and Genomics are distinct but coupled identities; Genomics adds genome-scale technology, data and computation dimensions.
- **FCR2C-7 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Information Science and Librarianship are overlapping but distinct: one centers inquiry into information organization/flow/use, the other includes professional/institutional information-access practice and infrastructure.
