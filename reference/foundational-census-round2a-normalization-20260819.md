# Foundational Census Round 2A — Identity / Topology Normalization

> Non-authoritative. All canonical root admissions remain **NOT_ADMITTED**.

## Logic

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_BRIDGE, METHODOLOGICAL_AXIS
- Placements: formal-inferential, mathematics, philosophy, computation
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: MSC treats mathematical logic and foundations as a major mathematical class whose internal structure includes philosophical logic, general logic, computability, set theory and proof theory. This supports one logic identity spanning multiple domains rather than a mathematics-only child.

## Computation and computing

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, BRIDGE_DOMAIN
- Placements: formal-inferential, engineering-intervention, information
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: Computability appears inside mathematical logic while ACM maintains a broad taxonomy for the computing field; computation therefore crosses formal theory and technological realization.

## Information theory

- Roles: FOUNDATIONAL_BRIDGE, METHODOLOGICAL_AXIS
- Placements: formal-inferential, computation, communication, statistics
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: IEEE defines information theory around mathematical underpinnings of information processing, transmission, storage and use, with coding, complexity, cryptography, learning and stochastic processes. It is broader than a communications-engineering subfield but not yet justified as a standalone root.

## Astronomy and astrophysics

- Roles: MAJOR_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, PHYSICAL_SPACE_BRIDGE
- Placements: space-cosmic, physics, planetary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: IAU organizes astronomy through nine divisions ranging from Fundamental Astronomy through planetary, stellar, interstellar, high-energy and galaxies/cosmology, demonstrating a first-class domain topology that should not be collapsed to one physics subnode.

## Cosmology

- Roles: MAJOR_SUBSPACE, PHYSICS_ASTRONOMY_BRIDGE
- Placements: astronomy, physics, space-cosmic
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: IAU places Galaxies and Cosmology within a dedicated division while high-energy/fundamental physics is separately represented; cosmology is therefore best treated as a major cross-boundary subspace, not a root by default.

## Earth science / geoscience

- Roles: MAJOR_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, SYSTEM_DOMAIN
- Placements: earth-environment, physical-material, planetary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: AGU explicitly spans Earth and space sciences and maintains many sections across atmosphere, biogeosciences, cryosphere, surface processes, geodesy and geomagnetism, supporting a first-class geoscience system domain.

## Geology

- Roles: MAJOR_SUBSPACE
- Placements: earth-science
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: Geology is substantial but current evidence is consistent with major subspace status inside a broader geoscience/Earth-system region.

## Geophysics

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, MAJOR_SUBSPACE
- Placements: earth-science, physics
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: AGU section structure exposes multiple explicitly physical Earth domains, supporting a physics↔Earth bridge identity rather than exclusive parentage.

## Atmospheric science and meteorology

- Roles: MAJOR_SUBSPACE, SYSTEM_DOMAIN
- Placements: earth-science, climate, physics
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: Atmospheric Sciences is an explicit AGU section and participates in broader Earth-system interactions; treat as major subspace with system-domain character.

## Oceanography

- Roles: MAJOR_SUBSPACE, SYSTEM_DOMAIN
- Placements: earth-science, climate, biology, chemistry, physics
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: Ocean science is inherently physical, chemical, biological and Earth-systemic; exclusive placement under one natural science would lose its topology.

## Climate science

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, SYSTEM_DOMAIN
- Placements: atmosphere, ocean, cryosphere, biosphere, earth-science
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: Climate cuts across atmospheric, oceanic, cryospheric, biogeochemical and surface-process regions and should be represented as a system bridge rather than a single-parent branch.

## Cognitive science

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, MAJOR_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE
- Placements: mind-cognition, psychology, neuroscience, linguistics, artificial-intelligence, philosophy, anthropology, education
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: The Cognitive Science Society explicitly promotes cognitive science as a discipline and names AI, linguistics, anthropology, psychology, neuroscience, philosophy and education as contributing areas. It must not be modeled as a psychology subclass.

## Psychology

- Roles: MAJOR_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, EMPIRICAL_MIND_DOMAIN
- Placements: mind-cognition, behavior, social-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: Psychology contains cognition-focused empirical science but also extends beyond cognition; current evidence supports an independent major domain identity, not reduction to cognitive science or social science.

## Neuroscience

- Roles: BRIDGE_DOMAIN, MAJOR_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE
- Placements: biology, medicine, mind-cognition, psychology
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: Neuroscience connects biological mechanisms, medical contexts and cognitive/psychological phenomena; preserve bridge status rather than force a single parent.

## Linguistics

- Roles: MAJOR_DOMAIN_CANDIDATE, BRIDGE_DOMAIN
- Placements: language, mind-cognition, social-boundary, formal-inferential, computation
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: LSA describes its mission as advancing the scientific study of language and publishes across semantics, pragmatics, phonology, syntax and related interfaces; cognitive science independently treats linguistics as a contributing field. Linguistics therefore warrants first-class identity with multiple bridges.

## Measurement and metrology

- Roles: METHODOLOGICAL_AXIS, EPISTEMIC_INFRASTRUCTURE
- Placements: methodology, physics, chemistry, biology, engineering, social-measurement-boundary
- Root admission: NOT_ADMITTED
- Rationale: VIM defines metrology as the science of measurement and its application, including all theoretical and practical aspects regardless of uncertainty or field. This is a cross-domain methodological axis, not an ordinary physics or engineering child.

## Findings

- **FCR2A-1 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Logic is better represented as a cross-domain foundational/methodological axis than as an ordinary child of mathematics.
- **FCR2A-2 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Computation and information require distinct but overlapping identities; neither should be collapsed into software engineering or communications.
- **FCR2A-3 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Astronomy deserves first-class major-domain representation, while cosmology is currently better modeled as a physics↔astronomy major bridge/subspace.
- **FCR2A-4 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Earth science/geoscience deserves first-class major-domain representation; atmosphere, ocean and geology are major subspaces, while climate is a cross-system bridge.
- **FCR2A-5 [SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE]** — Cognitive science is an interdisciplinary bridge-domain discipline, not a subclass of psychology; neuroscience and linguistics also require first-class identities with multi-domain placement.
- **FCR2A-6 [ESTABLISHED_IN_SCOPE]** — Measurement/metrology is a cross-domain methodological/epistemic axis and must not be forced under a single object-domain.
