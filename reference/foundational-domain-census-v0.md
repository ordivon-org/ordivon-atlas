# Foundational Knowledge Space Census v0

> **OPEN / NON-EXHAUSTIVE.** This is a discovery inventory, not a declaration of the final roots of knowledge.

## Formal and abstract foundations

Mathematics; Statistics and probability; Logic; Computation and theoretical computer science; Information theory; Systems and dynamical theory.

## Physical universe and matter

Physics; Astronomy and astrophysics; Cosmology; Chemistry; Materials science; Planetary science.

## Earth, ocean and atmosphere

Earth science / geoscience; Geology; Geophysics; Geochemistry; Atmospheric science and meteorology; Oceanography; Physical geography; Climate science.

## Life and biological organization

Biology; Molecular biology; Biochemistry; Genetics and genomics; Evolutionary biology; Ecology; Microbiology; Developmental biology; Physiology; Neuroscience.

## Mind, cognition and language

Philosophy; Linguistics; Cognitive science; Psychology; Philosophy of mind and cognition.

## Engineering, design and intervention

Engineering science; Mechanical engineering; Electrical and electronic engineering; Civil and structural engineering; Chemical engineering; Materials engineering; Computer engineering; Software engineering; Systems engineering; Control engineering; Bioengineering; Environmental engineering; Energy engineering.

## Humanities and historical-symbolic foundations

History; Philology; Classics; Religious studies; Aesthetics and art theory.

## Biomedical and agricultural boundary fields

Basic biomedical sciences; Medicine and health sciences; Agricultural science; Food science.

## Important boundary

The original Mathematics / Philosophy / Physics / Biology / Chemistry / Engineering set is only Seed Wave 0. Atlas must not infer that the foundational layer has six roots or that the families above are exhaustive.

Social, political, legal and institutional fields are intentionally later-wave domains, but the census stays open to bridge/boundary fields discovered before then.
