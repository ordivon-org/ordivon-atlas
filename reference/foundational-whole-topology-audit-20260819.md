## Provisional topology grammar

- **DOMAIN** — Stable area of inquiry organized around an object/regime/conceptual/temporal/system field.
- **MAJOR_SUBSPACE** — Large internally coherent region within or across domains; not presumed root.
- **BRIDGE** — Identity whose stable meaning intrinsically connects two or more domains.
- **AXIS** — Cross-cutting organizing dimension such as process, method, scale, measurement, design or integration.
- **INFRASTRUCTURE** — Research/epistemic capability enabling inquiry without being an object-domain itself.
- **APPLIED_TRANSLATIONAL** — Intervention/professional/realization domain that consumes foundational knowledge and may contain foundational cores.
- **COMPOSITE** — Institutionally stable field constructed from several distinct epistemic domains or methods.
